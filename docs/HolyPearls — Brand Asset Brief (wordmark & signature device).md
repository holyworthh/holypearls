# HolyPearls — Brand Asset Brief: wordmark & signature device

**Status:** Open. This needs brand input. **Created:** 26 Sep 2026

## The problem
The Signal direction (Geist, white and cool neutrals, signal red) is deliberately neutral so that products and photography carry the page. The trade-off is that nothing on the page is *ownable* yet. With a text-only wordmark, the site could belong to any modern SaaS company. Character now has to come from one or two brand assets, used consistently, rather than from the typeface.

## What we need
1. **Wordmark.** "HolyPearls" set as a drawn wordmark, not typed in Geist. It must work at 20px in the header and at 200px+ on signage and garments. It also has to embroider cleanly: no hairlines, and a minimum stroke of about 1.2 mm at 60 mm width.
2. **Symbol or monogram.** For the favicon, social avatar, garment chest marks, packaging tape and proof stamps.
3. **Signature device.** A graphic element the site repeats. The strongest candidate is already in the system: the **production ticket / registration mark** (`HP/Graphic/*`). Formalising it as a brand device, rather than a UI decoration, would give HolyPearls a recognisable "made and checked" stamp across web, packaging, labels and proofs.

## Constraints
- It must sit comfortably next to Geist at 600, and pass contrast on white, on Ink and on signal red.
- It must be reproducible in one colour (embroidery, screen print, stamp).
- No pearl clichés unless they are done with real wit.
- Signal red #D2311A is provisional; the brand work may confirm or change it. All colours are tokens, so a change is one edit.

## Deliverables
Primary wordmark, symbol, and one-colour and reversed versions; clear-space and minimum-size rules; SVG files; embroidery-tested version; the ticket/registration device spec.

## Where it lands on the board
Header and footer (`HP/Nav/Header`, `HP/Nav/Footer`), favicon, the homepage final band, proof and quote headers, packaging mock-ups, and garment chest marks in product imagery.
