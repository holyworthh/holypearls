## What was built

- **Branch:** `feature/t13-05b-staff-admin`. **PR:** see the tracker row.
- **Migration `0058_staff_invitations.sql`:** `staff_invitations` (identity). It holds:
  - the secret (only its SHA-256 is stored), the email and the name;
  - the permissions to grant, the status (PENDING, ACCEPTED, EXPIRED or REVOKED) and the expiry;
  - who invited and who accepted.
  - There's one pending invitation per email.
- **API (`identity/staff-admin.ts`, STAFF_ADMIN):**
  - **The staff list:** members with their email, permissions, MFA and status, plus open invitations.
  - **One member:**
    - their permissions and MFA;
    - the last MFA reset (when and by whom, from the audit log);
    - active sessions;
    - the active STAFF_ADMIN holders.
  - **Invite:** 7 days, one use, emailed with the new `staff_invitation` template; the secret travels only after `#`.
    - With permissions, it needs a recent MFA check (raising access, TECH-03 §9).
    - Refused if the person is already active staff, or already has an invitation waiting.
  - **Cancel an invitation.**
  - **Deactivate:**
    - Access ends at once, every session is signed out, and history is kept.
    - Not yourself, and never a holder that would leave fewer than two active STAFF_ADMIN ("Ada Okafor and Tunde Bello are the only STAFF_ADMIN…").
  - **Accept (on the customer site, `/v1/staff-invitations/preview` and `/accept`):**
    - Needs a signed-in account with the invited email, verified.
    - It creates the staff profile, or reactivates a deactivated one, with the invited permissions (grants record the inviter).
    - The answer carries the admin sign-in address, where MFA is set up next.
  - **The STAFF_ADMIN floor is race-free.** Removing STAFF_ADMIN (the existing revoke route, now in a transaction) and deactivating both count holders under one advisory lock. Two removals at once can't both pass.
  - **Unchanged:** granting, revoking and MFA reset stay as built in T00-07, with MFA and audit.
  - **OpenAPI:** documented.
- **Customer site `/invitations`:** one page for both kinds (TECH-03 §11). A secret that isn't a workspace invitation is tried as a staff one: "Join the HolyPearls staff" with the permissions. The page then guides through sign-in or creating an account, then verifying, accepting, and "Sign in to the admin".
- **Admin:**
  - **`/staff` (frame 122 `rbymz`):**
    - NAME, PERMISSIONS (three, then "+N"), MFA ("On" or "Reset — not set up") and STATUS (ACTIVE, DEACTIVATED, or PENDING for invitations).
    - "Invite staff" (email, name, permissions, with the step-up dialog), "Invitation sent", and "Cancel invitation".
    - The empty, error and 403 states.
  - **`/staff/[id]` (frame 122 `pplkz`):**
    - The header with the email, status, and the "MFA was reset … by …" note.
    - "Deactivate" (with the confirm dialog) and "Save permissions".
    - All 17 permissions as checkboxes. Saving grants and revokes one at a time, grants first, with step-up.
    - "Can't remove STAFF_ADMIN" when the floor blocks it.
    - **Security:** MFA and sessions, and "Reset MFA" behind "I confirmed …'s identity by phone" plus step-up.

## Permission rules added

- **Every `/v1/admin/staff` route:** STAFF_ADMIN (Finance gets 403, a customer 404).
- **Inviting with permissions:** also recent MFA.
- **Deactivating:** another person, and never below two active STAFF_ADMIN.
- **Accepting:** the invited, verified email only (WRONG_ACCOUNT, VERIFY_EMAIL_FIRST).

## Differences from the design or PRD, and why

1. **Accepting happens on the customer site's `/invitations` page,** as TECH-03 §11 describes for both kinds. The admin serves no sign-up (ADR-017), and the invitee needs an account first. MFA is then set up at the first admin sign-in.
2. **All 17 permissions are listed.** Frame 122 shows 16 (no ORGANIZATION_APPROVE).
3. **Saving permissions sends one change at a time** through the existing grant and revoke routes. If one fails (for example the STAFF_ADMIN floor), changes before it stay saved, and the page reloads to show what was saved.
4. **The phone check is a confirmation on the screen.** The API records the reset and who did it, but not the checkbox.
5. **Deactivated staff come back only by a new invitation.** Frame 122 shows no reactivate button.
