## Permission rules added

- **Viewing:** an ACTIVE member sees templates for their locations. All-locations templates are seen by everyone; drafts only by `MANAGE_BRAND`. Another location's template, or not being a member, is 404.
- **Create, edit, submit and archive:** `MANAGE_BRAND` (Owner and Admin); others get 403.
- **Sources:** only your own saved designs, or ordered items in your locations. Someone else's design is 404.
- **Assets:** only this organization's production-ready versions.
- **Locations:** a template's locations sit inside the creator's. All locations needs All locations (no escalation, TECH-03 §7).
- **Staff approval:** `STAFF_ADMIN`, frame 117's stated fallback. Customers get 404; staff without it get 403.

## Differences from the design or PRD, and why

1. **"Order" and "Order From Template" (frames 29, 68):** ordering is T11-01c. Cards open the detail, and the detail has no order button yet.
2. **"Create Group Collection" (frame 29):** groups are T12-01.
3. **"Last ordered" and "Historical orders" (frames 29, 68):** these need template orders (T11-01c). Cards show the product and locations instead.
4. **The thumbnail strip and colour/garment rows (frame 29):** the detail shows the product's main photo and the fields the configuration actually has. Frame 29's polo rows (thread, supplier route) appear when a garment template is made from a garment configuration.
5. **Create (frame 117 `TbBTL`):** a dialog in the workspace for Owners and Admins, rather than a staff screen. See Questions.
6. **Location and status filters on the index (frame 68):** templates are already limited to the member's locations, so only "Show archived" is offered. A location filter can follow when organizations have many templates.
7. **A field's reason is hidden when it repeats the value** ("Chosen per order" twice).

## Questions / conflicts found

1. **Who creates templates:**
   - Frame 117's create step says "Staff creates on the organization's request".
   - Frames 29 and 68 ("Only brand admins can change these", "Finish setup" on a draft) and TECH-03 §7 (Owner and Admin manage templates) put it with the organization.
   - PRD-05 doesn't say.
   - Built for Owners and Admins, with HolyPearls approving every template. **Owner to confirm.** If staff should also create on request, that's a small follow-up on the admin side.
2. **Template statuses beyond TECH-01 §4:**
   - **APPROVED or NEEDS_REVIEW → DRAFT on edit:** frame 29 says "Any change sends the template back for review", and the template must not stay approved after a change.
   - **AWAITING_APPROVAL → DRAFT:** frame 117's "Return to DRAFT".
   - **AWAITING_APPROVAL → NEEDS_REVIEW:** a version retired while the template waits.
   - **Proposed TECH-01 §4 amendment:** "Any change → DRAFT; AWAITING_APPROVAL → DRAFT (returned by HolyPearls); AWAITING_APPROVAL → NEEDS_REVIEW (a linked version retired)".
3. **"Or a product rule changed" (TECH-01 §4):** templates aren't moved to NEEDS_REVIEW automatically when a product rule changes. The staff check "Configuration is producible" blocks approval of an unpublished product. Suggested follow-up: a catalogue change hook, or a check when ordering (T11-01c).
4. **Staff permission for template approval:** frame 117 says "permission to be decided". Built with `STAFF_ADMIN`, as for asset review (T11-01 Question 2). **Owner to choose,** for both at once.
