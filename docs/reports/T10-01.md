### Data model (migration `0040_organizations.sql`)

All IDs are UUIDv7, and every table has `created_at`. Editable tables also have `updated_at` and a `version` column.

| Table | Module | Columns and constraints |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| `organizations` | organizations | `name`; `archived_at` (null at launch) |
| `locations` | organizations | `organization_id`, `name`, `city`; unique (`organization_id`, lower(`name`)); unique (`id`, `organization_id`), the target of the composite foreign keys below |
| `memberships` | organizations | `organization_id`, `user_id`, `role` (OWNER, ADMIN, BUYER, LOCATION_MANAGER, APPROVER, VIEWER), `status` (ACTIVE, DEACTIVATED), `all_locations` boolean, `deactivated_at`, `version`; unique (`organization_id`, `user_id`); index (`user_id`, `status`) |
| `membership_locations` | organizations | `membership_id`, `organization_id`, `location_id`; a composite foreign key (`location_id`, `organization_id`) → `locations`, so the database itself refuses a location from another organization |
| `membership_permissions` | organizations | `membership_id`, `permission` (enum below); unique pair |
| `invitations` | identity (TECH-01 §2) | `kind` (ORGANIZATION; staff invitations come with T13-05), `secret_hash` (unique, SHA-256 of 32 random bytes), `email_normalized`, `status`, `expires_at`, `organization_id`, `role`, `all_locations`, `invited_by_user_id`, `accepted_by_user_id`, `accepted_at`, `revoked_at`; one PENDING invitation per (organization, email) |
| `invitation_locations` | identity | `invitation_id`, `organization_id`, `location_id`, with the same composite foreign key |

Other module tables get no `organization_id` yet. Orders, projects and the rest join organizations in T10-03 and T11.

### Permissions (TECH-03 §7)

Organization permissions, one enum shared by the contracts and the database:

- `VIEW_RECORDS` (Buyers don't hold it; they see only their own records)
- `CREATE_REQUESTS`
- `APPROVE_REQUESTS`
- `ACCEPT_QUOTES`
- `MANAGE_BRAND`
- `MANAGE_COLLECTIONS`
- `MANAGE_STORES`
- `MANAGE_TEAM`
- `VIEW_BILLING`
- `EXPORT_DATA`

Role presets fill `membership_permissions` exactly as the table in §7 does.

- **Transfer ownership is tied to the Owner role.** It is not a grantable permission, so the last-owner rule stays meaningful.
- **"All locations" is a membership flag,** not a permission.

Rules (all in `organizations/policies.ts`; controllers only call them):

- **Adjusting one member's permissions** away from their role preset is for Owners only (TECH-03 §7: "the Owner can adjust per member").
- **No escalation:** nobody can grant a permission or a location they don't hold themselves. A team manager limited to Lekki can only invite to Lekki, only manage members whose locations are all inside theirs, and can't grant All locations. (Question 3: this means Admins can't create Approvers, because Admins don't hold `APPROVE_REQUESTS`.)
- **Owner changes are for Owners only.** Only an Owner can make someone an Owner, change another Owner or deactivate an Owner.
- **Last owner:** the last ACTIVE Owner can't be deactivated, downgraded or have their role changed (B2B-FR-092). This is checked inside the transaction with a row lock on the organization's owner memberships, so two simultaneous changes can't both pass.
- **Location management** (add a location, edit its name and city) needs `MANAGE_TEAM` plus All locations. The permission table doesn't name this, so it's recorded under Questions.

### Who can see what (TECH-03 §12, 403 or 404)

- **ActorContext.** It gains `organizationMember(orgId)`. It loads the ACTIVE membership with its permissions and locations once per request, memoized on the request. Nothing is cached in the session, so a deactivated member loses access on their very next request (B2B-FR-093).
- **Not a member, or deactivated:** every `/v1/organizations/:orgId/...` route answers **404**.
- **A member without the permission:** **403**, for example a Viewer posting an invitation.
- **Scoped queries:** `findLocationForMember`, `listLocationsForMember`, `findMembershipForManager` and `listTeamForManager`. Location scope is applied in SQL from the membership's locations. Records outside the member's locations are **404**.
- **Team page:** needs `MANAGE_TEAM`. A manager limited to some locations sees members who share one of those locations, plus the members with All locations.
- **Locations:** a member sees only their own locations (all of them with All locations). The location detail lists that location's members.

### High-risk actions (TECH-03 §9, B2B-FR-091)

These need the permission, a confirmation, an email code in the last 24 hours (`requireRecentEmailCode`, purpose VERIFY_ACTION) and an audit entry:

- **Raising someone's permissions:** a role change, permission adjustment or location change that adds any permission or location. Reactivating a member also counts.
- **Changing the final Owner:** ownership transfer.

The server enforces the confirmation. A raising change without `confirmed: true` gets `RULE_FAILED CONFIRMATION_REQUIRED`, with the permissions and locations it would add, and the web app shows that in the Pen's confirmation dialog. Lowering someone's permissions or deactivating them needs a confirmation but no email code. Both are audited.

### Invitations (TECH-03 §11, B2B-FR-090)

- **The secret** is 32 random bytes (base64url), stored as a SHA-256 hash. It expires after 7 days (`INVITATION_VALID_DAYS`, a setting in contracts) and works once.
- **Sending:** the `organization_invitation` email carries the link, so it is sealed, wiped after sending and never resent, like the sign-in link.
- **Opening the link:** `/invitations/[token]` is served with `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`, and the secret is never logged.
- **Accepting** needs a signed-in account whose **verified** email equals the invited email. Other cases are refused, each with its own state: signed out (sign in or sign up, then return), wrong account, unverified email, expired, revoked, already used.
- **Re-inviting a deactivated member** reactivates them with the new role and locations when they accept.
- **Status changes:** revoking sets REVOKED. The existing daily clean-up job marks PENDING invitations past expiry as EXPIRED, and accepting checks the expiry anyway.
- **Owners aren't invited.** Owners are made by an Owner after the person joins (question 4).

### Ownership transfer

An Owner picks an ACTIVE member, who becomes Owner. The Owner can keep the Owner role or step down to Admin. Both happen in one transaction, behind the confirmation, the email code and an audit entry.

### API (Zod contracts in `packages/contracts/src/organizations.ts`, documented in OpenAPI)

| Route | Who |
| :-- | :-- |
| `GET /v1/me/organizations` | signed in: their ACTIVE memberships |
| `GET /v1/organizations/:orgId` | member: the organization, my role, permissions and locations (for display only) |
| `GET /v1/organizations/:orgId/locations`, `GET …/locations/:locationId` | member, location-scoped |
| `POST …/locations`, `PATCH …/locations/:locationId` | `MANAGE_TEAM` + All locations |
| `GET …/members` (members and invitations) | `MANAGE_TEAM`, location-scoped |
| `POST …/invitations`, `POST …/invitations/:id/revoke` | `MANAGE_TEAM` |
| `PATCH …/members/:membershipId` (role, permissions, locations, `version`) | `MANAGE_TEAM`; Owners for permission adjustments and Owner changes |
| `POST …/members/:membershipId/deactivate`, `…/reactivate` | `MANAGE_TEAM` |
| `POST …/ownership-transfer` | Owner |
| `GET /v1/invitations/preview` (token in a header), `POST /v1/invitations/accept` | anyone / signed in with the matching verified email |
| `GET /v1/admin/organizations?q=`, `GET /v1/admin/organizations/:id` | staff with `ORGANIZATION_SUPPORT` (read only) |

Writes take idempotency keys like the other routes. Membership edits check `version`.

### Workspace requests (owner decision 2026-10-08)

- **Requesting:** from `/account`, a signed-in customer with a verified email requests a workspace with a company name, locations (name and city, at least one), a rough team size and an optional note. Unverified accounts see "Verify your email first". There is one open request per person.
- **The queue:** an admin queue with Approve and Decline (a reason is required). Approving creates the organization and its locations, and makes the requester an ACTIVE Owner with All locations, in one transaction. Every decision is audited, and the requester is emailed either way.
- **New table:** `organization_requests` (PENDING → APPROVED | DECLINED | WITHDRAWN).
- **Guard: a new staff permission `ORGANIZATION_APPROVE`** (approved by the owner), not `ORGANIZATION_SUPPORT`. OPS-FR-081 defines the support view as read-only, so every support person can look at a customer's organization safely. Approving creates an organization and hands out an Owner role, which is a write that grants customer permissions. Keeping it a separate permission means support staff stay read-only, and the owner chooses who can approve. This needs a one-line addition to TECH-03 §8, which I'll propose in the report rather than edit (CLAUDE.md).
- **Screens:** no Pen frames. The request form is built from the account cards (frame 92) and the queue from the admin list and `HP/Admin/DecisionBar`. Both will be listed as screens without a Pen frame.
- **Recording the decision:** in this report and as a proposed `ATTENTION.md` / owner-decisions note, because no PRD covers it.
