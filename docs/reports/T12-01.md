## What was built

- **Branch:** `feature/t12-01-group-collections`. **PR:** see the tracker row.
- **Migration `0046_group_collections.sql`, `group_collections`:**
  - The organization and location (composite foreign key), title and status, and the template (if any) and source design.
  - The allowed participant fields, the deadline, payment mode, delivery mode with its central location or branches, and the unsure-size rule.
  - The share link's secret, hashed for lookup and sealed with `DATA_ENCRYPTION_KEY` so the organizer can copy it again.
  - A saved copy of the source (TECH-01 §5), the creator, when it opened, and `version`.
- **Contracts (`groups.ts`):** statuses (all eight from B2B-FR-050), participant fields, modes, the collection list and detail (with `availableFields` and reasons), create, update, actions, and the participant's invite view.
- **API (`groups/collections.ts`, a new groups module):**
  - **Create** from an approved template the member can see (for a location the template allows), or from their own saved design. `MANAGE_COLLECTIONS` for the location.
  - **Which fields can be offered** (B2B-FR-052):
    - **Template:** only the fields it marks buyer-editable. Sizes, personalisation, quantity and the colour option each show "… is locked by the template" otherwise.
    - **Saved design:** what the product offers. "This product has no sizes" for non-garments, and colour only when the product has a colour choice.
  - **Update** while DRAFT or PAUSED: fields (only the allowed ones), deadline, who pays, delivery (central location, approved branches or home) and the unsure-size rule.
  - **Open:** DRAFT or PAUSED → OPEN. Needs a future deadline ("Choose a deadline in the future.") and a template that's still approved. The share link is made the first time and kept on re-open.
  - **Pause:** OPEN → PAUSED. **Cancel:** any status before an order draft exists. Every change is audited.
  - **`POST /v1/collections/invite`:** what a participant sees on opening the link. No account needed, and the secret goes in the body, not the URL (rate-limited like guest links).
    - It returns the title, organization, product, status (OPEN, PAUSED, or CLOSED once the deadline passes), the allowed fields, the modes and the branches. Nothing about other participants or the organization's records.
    - Drafts and cancelled collections don't exist to the link.
  - **Templates** export `templateSourceConfiguration`, so the groups module never reads template tables (TECH-00 §5).
  - **OpenAPI:** the 7 routes are documented.
- **Customer site:**
  - **"Groups" in the sidebar** (and on the More page): the list with status, product, location and closing date.
  - **New group collection, step 1:** a name, the location (the person's own), and what it starts from: approved templates and their saved designs.
  - **Setup, step 2 (frame 132):**
    - The status line ("Participants can't see it yet" or the paused message).
    - The share link with Copy link, masked.
    - "Starts from" and "What participants fill in", with unavailable fields disabled and the reason shown.
    - Delivery address and Payment follow the chosen modes.
    - "Deadline and modes": deadline, who pays, delivery with the central location or branches, and the "Not sure of my size" rule.
    - Open collection, Save draft, Pause, Open again and Cancel collection.
  - **Template detail:** "Create Group Collection" (frame 29) for people who can manage collections.
  - **The garment configurator's "Ordering for a team?" note** (T03-05) now links to "Start a Group Collection". It opens step 1 in the person's workspace with the design chosen, or explains that collections are for business workspaces.

## Permission rules added

- **Create and change:** `MANAGE_COLLECTIONS` for the collection's location. Viewers and Approvers get 403; a location the member doesn't have is 404.
- **Source:** only the member's own saved design (someone else's is 404), or an approved template visible to them and allowed for the location.
- **Seeing:** the creator, and members with `MANAGE_COLLECTIONS` or `VIEW_RECORDS` for the location. Others get 404. The share link is only shown to people who manage the collection.
- **Participants:** the invite view only. Submissions come in T12-01b.

## Differences from the design or PRD, and why

1. **Step 1 is its own page** ("NEW GROUP COLLECTION · STEP 1"), not shown in frame 132, which only shows step 2. Frame 132's "STEP 2 OF 3" becomes "step 2 of 2": the third step (participants' progress) belongs to T12-03's organizer dashboard.
2. **"Delivery address" and "Payment" checkboxes (frame 132):** shown, but they follow the delivery and payment choices rather than being set by hand. B2B-FR-063 asks for a home address only when items go to each participant, and B2B-FR-064 shows payment only when participants pay.
3. **The share link reads `/g#…`** (the secret after `#`, like other guest links, so it never reaches server logs). The participant page at `/g` arrives with T12-01b. Until then the link shows only to organizers.
4. **Approved branches** are chosen from the locations the organizer can see.
5. **A possible flaky browser check:** on 375 px, a control that happens to sit under the sticky bottom tabs when a long page loads can fail axe's `target-size` check. That happened once with a longer list of sources; it passed on re-run. Suggested follow-up: a shell-level fix for content under the tabs (for example bottom padding equal to the bar's height).
