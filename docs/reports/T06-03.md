# T06-03 — Proof comments, pins and changes after approval

Status: IN REVIEW (pending CI and merge).

**Branch:** `feature/t06-03-proof-comments-changes`.
- **PRD:** FUL-FR-005, 007; OPS-FR-052, 054.
- **TECH:** TECH-01 §4 (change requests), TECH-04 §11 (pins).
- Built under the owner's standing instruction of 2 October 2026; the plan is recorded below.

## Plan as built
1. **Pins and comments:**
   - Comments on the whole proof, and pins placed on the artwork. Pins are stored as positions relative to the image (0–1 across and down) on a view, numbered per version.
   - Every pin also appears in the numbered text list, and comments stay on their version.
   - Staff reply in the same thread, and replies never change the proof status.
2. **Changes after approval:**
   - A customer can ask to change an approved proof. The stage is recorded: BEFORE_PRODUCTION, or AFTER_PRODUCTION_START once the item is in production.
   - Staff start a review, then decide, recording the **cost and timing impact** and a note.
   - Accepting can start a new proof version (the approved one is kept) or note a quote revision.
   - Declining keeps the approved version as it is.

## What was built
- **Data (migration `0032_proof_pins_changes`):**
  - pin fields on `proof_comments`: x and y (0–1, with a database check), view, number (unique per version) and the parent for replies;
  - a new `change_requests` table: order, proof, stage, status, description, cost impact in kobo, timing impact in days, outcome, staff note, who decided and when.
- **Customer viewer (Pen 125 plus the T06-01 viewer):**
  - **Add pin:** click the artwork, or use the keyboard (arrow keys move the marker, Enter goes to the comment, Esc cancels). Then "Pin N comment" and **Save pin**.
  - **Pins:** drawn at their relative position, so they stay put at every width and zoom.
  - **Comments tab:** "Comments are saved on V2", a numbered list (pin number, view, text), staff replies under each, and **Add a comment on the whole proof**.
  - **Older versions are read-only:** the API refuses comments on them (OLDER_VERSION).
  - **Once approved:** "Need a change?", then **Request a change**, which replies "We'll review your change and tell you if it affects the cost or delivery date".
- **The order page** lists "Changes you asked for", with status and the staff note.
- **Staff:**
  - Proofs → **Change requests** tab: Start review, then decide (accept, or decline keeping the approved version), the outcome (new proof version, quote revision, nothing), cost change in ₦, timing change in days, and a note for the customer;
  - the proof preparation page has **Reply to pin N** threads.
  - Everything is audited.

## Tests (added to `proofs/proofs.test.ts`; 12 in the file)
- **Pins and comments:**
  - pins are numbered 1 and 2 per version, with their relative positions kept, beside a whole-proof comment;
  - an out-of-range pin is refused;
  - a staff reply sits in the pin's thread, and the proof stays AWAITING_APPROVAL.
- **Older versions are read-only.**
- **Change before production:**
  - the stage is recorded, and a second open request is refused;
  - staff review, then accept with ₦15,000 and 2 days recorded;
  - **the approved V1 is kept** and a V2 draft is opened (REVISION_IN_PROGRESS);
  - the order shows the decision.
- **Change after production started:**
  - recorded as AFTER_PRODUCTION_START;
  - **declining keeps the approved version**;
  - 403 for staff without PROOF_MANAGE.
- **A change request on a proof that isn't approved is refused.**

## Questions and conflicts
1. **"Revise the quote" as an outcome** records the decision. Re-opening an accepted quote for a new version and re-acceptance isn't built: the accepted quote is final (QUOTE-FR-001), and a priced change after acceptance needs its own flow. Suggestion: an "order adjustment" payment for the recorded cost impact, with T13-05.
2. **The stage** follows the item's production status. Items move into production from T07-01, so until then every change is BEFORE_PRODUCTION.
