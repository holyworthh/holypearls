- **API (catalogue and configuration):**
  - **`GET /v1/admin/pricing/products` (PRICING_MANAGE):** every product with whether its rules are set, the price in force now and whether a later one is scheduled. The catalogue's own product list needs CATALOGUE_MANAGE, which a pricing manager may not hold.
  - **`GET /v1/admin/pricing/products/:id/rules`** now also returns the product (name, slug, buying mode, status) and its options and values, for the compatibility and add-on choices.
  - **OpenAPI:** updated.
  - **No new migrations.**
- **Admin (`/rules`, "Rules & prices"):**
  - **The list:** product, buying mode, price now (with "new price scheduled"), and whether rules are set.
  - **Product page tabs (frame 107):**
    - **Product rules:**
      - The minimum and what happens below it (block, manual review, surcharge), and the automatic-price limit.
      - Lead time and rush, and priority rush with surcharges.
      - Artwork later, samples (offer and price), and proofs (with skip on exact reorder).
      - Compatibility: when one choice is made, some values of another are not allowed or allowed after review, with the customer's message.
    - **Price rules:** every version with its date and CURRENT, SCHEDULED or PAST. A new version takes a date (Lagos time; now or later), how it's priced (per unit, per pack or fixed), currency, base price, quantity tiers, add-on charges, exact or estimate, VAT included, and a note. Its fields start from the latest version.
    - **Price-state rules:** how the calculator decides CONSULTATION_REQUIRED, QUOTE_REQUIRED, ESTIMATE_READY or PRICE_READY, taken from the calculator itself.
    - **VAT:** every rate with its date and status, and a new rate from a date.
  - **Shared helper:** the naira-to-kobo helper was copied in two admin files. It now lives in `(shell)/format.ts` with a reverse for prefilling.

## Differences from the design or PRD, and why

1. **No MFA step-up for price changes (frame 107 states).** TECH-03 §9 lists the high-risk actions, and price changes aren't among them. The higher source is followed; every change is still audited. **Owner to confirm** whether price changes should be added to TECH-03 §9.
2. **Price-state rules are explained, not edited.** Frame 107 shows an ordered rule list ("first match wins") with rows like "Sourced garment not in the catalogue". The calculator (T03-01) decides the state from the buying mode, the rules, compatibility and the price version's own state; there is no separate rule table. The tab explains exactly that.
3. **Current versus new comparison table (frame 107 JElIg):** the versions are listed with their tiers. The side-by-side "change %" column isn't shown.
4. **Compatibility as rules, not a decoration × placement matrix (frame 107 pGEKn).** The API stores general "when … then …" rules. Decoration placements are garment data, which is T13-01b.
5. **Overlapping tiers (frame 107 states):** tiers are entered by starting quantity, so they can't overlap. Two tiers with the same start are refused.
6. **Product rules shows a stacked form,** with the compatibility panel beside it on wide screens. The frame's toggles are checkboxes.

## Amendment (2026-10-19): price changes need MFA

**Branch:** `fix/t13-01-price-change-step-up`.

PRD-06 §18 lists price changes among the high-risk staff actions: the right permission, a confirmation step, an MFA check within the last 15 minutes, and an audit entry. PRD-06 ranks above TECH-03 (CLAUDE.md source order), so frame 107's step-up was right, and the first difference above ("no MFA step-up") is withdrawn.

**Changes:**
- A new price version and a new VAT rate (which changes every price) need an authenticator check within the last 15 minutes. Without it the API answers `RECENT_MFA_REQUIRED`.
- The Price rules and VAT forms then open the step-up dialog ("Schedule a new price version", "Change the VAT rate") and save once it's confirmed.
- Product rules (minimums, lead times) and compatibility aren't prices and don't ask.
- **Test (`configuration/rule-changes.test.ts`):** with the session's MFA check 16 minutes old, both are refused with `RECENT_MFA_REQUIRED`, and nothing is saved.

The question about TECH-03 §9 is answered by PRD-06 §18. **Suggested doc change:** add "price changes" to TECH-03 §9's list so the two agree.
