## What was built

- **Branch:** `feature/t13-01b-garment-data`. **PR:** https://github.com/holyworthh/holypearls/pull/112.
- **Migration `0054_garment_data.sql`:**
  - **`fabrics`:** key (matching the value key products use in their fabric option), name, weight or detail, colours, size range, staff-only supplier code, ACTIVE or ARCHIVED, and `version`.
  - **`placement_blocks`:** a decoration method (EMBROIDERY or PRINT), a placement, and what the customer is told. The migration seeds the rule the configurator used to hard-code: no embroidery across the full front or back, with the same messages.
  - **`configurations.previous_total_minor`:** the total a repriced draft had before.
- **API:**
  - **Garment data (`catalogue/garment-data.ts`, CATALOGUE_MANAGE, audited):**
    - `GET /v1/admin/garment-data`: fabrics with the products that use them (their fabric or material option offers the key), the blocked placements, and how each product is made and decorated.
    - `POST` and `PUT /fabrics` (keys unique; archive instead of delete) and `PUT /placements` (the whole set).
  - **Data-driven placement conflicts:** `placementConflicts` takes the blocked placements as data. A blocked method on a placement is refused with its message, and the fix is the other method ("Print this area instead" or "Embroider this area instead"). The hard-coded full-front and full-back embroidery check is gone; it lives in the data.
  - **Price updated (OPS-FR-130):**
    - When a draft is opened, its last price is checked against the price version, product rules and VAT in force. If any changed, it is repriced, and the total it had is kept.
    - The draft shows `priceUpdate {wasMinor, nowMinor}` until the customer changes the design.
    - Drafts sent for a quote, in carts or ordered are never repriced here: carts reprice at checkout (T04), and orders keep their saved copies.
  - **Scheduled changes (PRICING_MANAGE):** `GET /v1/admin/pricing/scheduled` lists price versions that haven't started, with how many drafts and cart items each will touch.
  - **OpenAPI:** the new routes are documented.
- **Admin:**
  - **"Garment data"** (`/garment-data`, under Catalogue; frame 118 DGylb):
    - **Fabrics & colours:** fabric, colours, sizes, used by, and the staff-only supplier code, with an add and change form (archive keeps history) and the "Internal fields" note.
    - **Decorations & placements:** a matrix of embroidery and print by placement. Untick to block, then say what the customer is told.
    - **Garment sourcing:** how each product is made and decorated, linking to the product to change it.
  - **Rules & prices** shows "Scheduled changes" (frame 118 RW4ec): product, date, drafts to be repriced on next open, and cart items that will show a price update.
  - **Shared stylesheet:** the admin form layout stylesheet is now `(shell)/form-layout.module.css`, shared by both sections.
- **Customer site:** both configurators show "Price updated · Was ₦… · now ₦… for N × product. Your choices are unchanged." above the price.

## Differences from the design or PRD, and why

1. **Colour retirement with an effective date (frame 118 "Piqué cotton colour Red retired" on 1 Nov):** fabric changes apply when saved. Products still offer colours through their own options, so retiring a colour there is done on the product. Scheduling garment changes is a suggested follow-up.
2. **"Used by"** is worked out from the products whose fabric or material option offers the fabric's key, not from a separate link.
3. **"Size ranges" is a field on each fabric, not its own tab.** Sizes are chosen per order from the garment sizes (XS–4XL).
4. **Can't delete (frame 118 "IN USE · CAN'T DELETE"):** fabrics can't be deleted at all; they're archived, and history is kept.
5. **Scheduled changes show price versions only.** Rule and VAT changes take effect when saved, so there's nothing to schedule. The frame's "14 will be repriced on next open" and "3 carts show a price update" are the counts per scheduled version.
6. **The Price updated notice clears on the customer's next change to the design** (choices, quantity, rush, artwork choice, sourcing, decoration, sizes or placements). Uploading a file or a names list is saved separately and doesn't clear it.
