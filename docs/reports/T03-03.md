## What was built

### Configuration drafts (configuration module; migration `0013_configurations`)
- **`configurations`**:
  - product, status (DRAFT, IN_CART, QUOTE_REQUESTED, ABANDONED)
  - owner (account, or a hashed browser device key), customer (set when a guest asks for the link)
  - quantity, rush, artwork choice (upload, design help, later), the step the customer was on
  - the last saved price result, and a version
- **`configuration_selections`** (TECH-01 §2): one row per answered option, in the flow's order.
- **Ownership** (TECH-03 §5, §12). A draft opens only for:
  - the account that started it
  - the browser that started it: an HttpOnly `hp.device` cookie, stored as SHA-256
  - a guest whose emailed link targets that draft
  - Anyone else gets **404**, the same as a missing draft (tested with another customer, another browser and another guest).
- **Autosave with versions** (COM-FR-012, TECH-02 §5). Each change sends the version it started from. An older version gets 409 `VERSION_CONFLICT` with the latest draft, and the page loads it and says so. The page saves one change at a time, so its own quick changes never conflict. Every save re-prices the draft with the T03-01 calculator and keeps the saved result.
- **Keep compatible answers** (COM-FR-011):
  - Changing an earlier answer keeps later answers that still fit.
  - If a "not allowed" rule would remove a later answer, the save is refused with 422 `ANSWERS_CONFLICT` and the list. The page shows "This change affects later answers" with the reasons: Keep my earlier choice, or Remove and continue (resend with `confirmRemovals`).
  - A newly chosen value that isn't allowed with an earlier one is refused with the rule's message.
- **Save failures** (COM-FR-013): the work stays on screen, the page shows "Not saved — retrying", retries every 5 seconds, and offers "Retry now".
- **Emailed draft link:** `POST /v1/configurations/:id/email` (CONTACT limit). It creates or finds the customer, creates a CONFIGURATION guest link (90 days) and sends the existing `guest_link` email ("Your HolyPearls saved business cards design"). The link opens a guest session scoped to that draft; `/configure/drafts/:id` then opens the product page with the draft. The email carries a secret, so it can't be resent from admin (T01-02 rule).
- **API:** `POST /v1/configurations`, `GET/PATCH /v1/configurations/:id`, `POST /v1/configurations/:id/email`.

### Product page template (`/products/:slug`, Pen 21 · VSjt2, mobile 20 · XX8rq; COM-FR-001–005)
- Breadcrumb; **gallery** with thumbnails (COM-FR-003) and "Stock, finish and size are confirmed in your proof".
- Title, summary, **minimum order and lead time** facts, and the price-state chip ("PRICE READY · ORDER NOW", estimate, quote required).
- **Inline configurator** (COM-FR-040), one page that grows as you go, with numbered steps that never change (COM-FR-010):
  1. **Options:** each option as choice cards. Combinations that can't be made are **disabled before Review, with the reason** (COM-FR-042).
  2. **Quantity:** standard buttons with the per-unit price at each tier, plus a custom quantity (COM-FR-043).
  3. **Artwork (placeholder):** upload later, design help, or send it later. The upload screens come in T03-05.
  4. **Delivery (placeholder):** chosen at checkout, where cost and date are confirmed; coverage statement.
  5. **Review:** every choice with an **Edit** link back to its step (COM-FR-014).
- **Total bar next to the choices** (COM-FR-004/005): summary, **total and per unit including VAT**, reasons (for example below the minimum, estimate, review needed), save state, "Email me a link to this design" and the lead time.
- **Mobile sticky bar** (COM-FR-002): shows the price and a Review link while the total bar is off screen, and sits at the bottom so it never covers the field being filled in.
- Below: about the product, **how it works** (configure, artwork, proof, make and deliver) with the delivery statement, specifications, FAQs and related products.
- Consultative products show "Start a project" instead of a configurator; temporarily unavailable products say so.
- `?draft=` reopens a saved design for its owner. The public product API now includes the configurator data: options with labels and defaults, MOQ, the automatic limit, lead time, priced quantity presets, blocked combinations and currency.

## Differences from the design

1. **Paper imagery:** Pen shows a photo per paper stock. There are no stock photos yet (ATTENTION item 11), so papers are text cards.
2. **"Add to bag":** the cart is T04-01. The button reads "Continue to checkout" (or "Request a quote") and is disabled, with "Online checkout opens soon"; customers can email themselves the design.
3. **Artwork step:** Pen offers "Upload my design / Need design help / I'll send it later". The choice is saved, but the upload itself arrives with T03-05 (artwork per product).
4. **Delivery step:** the ticket's flow includes Delivery. Rates are T04-01, so the step explains that delivery is chosen at checkout.
5. **Quantity presets:** Pen shows 100, 500, 1,000 and More. The page shows the MOQ and each tier (100, 500, 1,000) plus a custom quantity field instead of "More".
6. **Per-card prices include VAT.** Pen's "₦150 / card" matches the price before VAT; the page shows ₦161.25 including VAT, so the per-card price and the total agree (COM-FR-005). See Questions.

## Questions / conflicts found

- **Prices with or without VAT:** Pen shows prices before VAT (₦150 per card, ₦52,500 total for 500). The page shows prices including VAT (7.5%, exclusive in the rules), so what customers see is what they pay. **Owner decision:** show VAT-inclusive prices (current), or before-VAT prices with "+ VAT"?
- **Guest drafts after signing in:** a draft started in a browser stays tied to that browser. When the customer signs in, it isn't moved to their account yet. That belongs with customer accounts and guest linking (T09-01).
- **Abandoned drafts:** the ABANDONED status exists, but nothing sets it yet. Suggest the daily clean-up marks drafts untouched for 90 days (the guest-link life) as ABANDONED (T04-01, with carts).
