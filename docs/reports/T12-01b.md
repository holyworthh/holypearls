## What was built

- **Branch:** `feature/t12-01b-participant-flow`. **PR:** https://github.com/holyworthh/holypearls/pull/100.
- **Migration `0047_participants.sql`:**
  - **`participants`:** collection, customer (a guest customer for their email), the account if they joined signed in, name, email, status (NOT_STARTED, IN_PROGRESS, SUBMITTED, NEEDS_CORRECTION, REMOVED), origin (`LINK`; CSV rows arrive in T12-03), and `version`.
  - **`participant_submissions`:** one per participant, with the answers, the organizer's correction note, and when they were sent.
- **Contracts (`groups.ts`):** participant statuses, answers (size, "not sure", personalisation up to 12 letters, quantity, colour, branch), join, and the participant's own view, save and submit.
- **API (`groups/participants.ts`):**
  - **`POST /v1/collections/join`** (share-link secret, name and email):
    - Only while the collection is OPEN and before its deadline (`COLLECTION_PAUSED` or `COLLECTION_CLOSED` otherwise; drafts and cancelled collections are 404).
    - Creates the participant, a guest link for their submission (emailed: "your answers for …"), and a guest session cookie for them alone. Nothing is looked up by email, so nobody reaches someone else's answers by typing their address.
  - **`GET /v1/participants/:id`:** only with that participant's guest session, or the account they joined with. Anyone else gets 404.
    - It shows their own answers and the collection as a participant may see it: title, organization, product, deadline, allowed fields, delivery mode and branches.
    - It never includes other people, counts or anything about price or payment.
  - **`PUT …/answers`:** answers are checked against the organizer's choices:
    - A field the collection doesn't ask for is refused.
    - Sizes come from the garment list, and "not sure" only when the organizer allows it.
    - A branch only from the approved ones, and none at all for central delivery.
    - Names are up to 12 letters.
  - **`POST …/submit`:**
    - Needs everything the collection asks for: a size or "not sure", a colour if asked, and the branch for branch delivery.
    - Sends a copy by email (`collection_submitted`) and marks the participant SUBMITTED.
    - Answers can be changed until the deadline.
  - **The organizer's collection shows how many have sent their answers.** The full dashboard is T12-03.
  - **OpenAPI:** the 4 routes are documented.
- **Customer site (no workspace chrome, as frame 31 says: the organization on top, "Made by HolyPearls for …" underneath):**
  - **`/g#secret`:**
    - The share link. It removes the secret from the address bar, then shows the collection, what it asks for, "Closes …", "Only you and your organiser see your answers" and "Nothing to pay".
    - Then your name and email, and Start.
    - Paused and closed links explain what happened and who to ask (frame 76 E).
  - **`/collections/submissions/[id]`:** one decision per screen.
    - Size buttons, with "I'm not sure: your organiser will help confirm it" when allowed (frame 76 A).
    - Name with a live preview and "No name, just the logo".
    - Colour, quantity, and branch ("It arrives with your branch's team delivery. No home address needed").
    - "Check and send" with Edit links, and "there's nothing to pay".
    - "You're in, …".
    - A correction from the organizer shows as "Please fix 1 thing" (frame 76 B; the organizer side is T12-03).

## Permission rules added

- **A participant reaches only their own submission:** their guest session (purpose COLLECTION_SUBMISSION, target their participant record) or the account they joined with. Another participant's id is 404, and the organizer's API needs a workspace account (401).
- **Joining** needs an OPEN collection before its deadline. Answers can only be saved or sent while it's open.

## Differences from the design or PRD, and why

1. **Name and email before the first step:** frame 31 goes straight from the intro to "What size are you?". A name and email are needed for the organizer to know who answered, and for the participant's copy and personal link. They're asked on the intro screen with Start.
2. **No "size guide" link:** products don't have size charts yet. The hint says to check a garment that fits or ask the organizer.
3. **Frame 76 C (participant pays) and D (home delivery)** are not built, as the frame itself and the owner's decision say.
4. **Organization identity:** the header shows the organization's initials. Workspaces don't have logos yet; they could use the primary brand asset later.
