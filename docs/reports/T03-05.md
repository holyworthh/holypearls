Status: DONE (pending CI and merge).

## What was built

### Data (migration `0014_garment_configuration`)
- `configurations`: sourcing (SOURCE_CUSTOMIZE, MAKE_FROM_SCRATCH, CUSTOMER_SUPPLIED, HELP_ME_CHOOSE), decoration (EMBROIDERY, PRINT, BOTH, RECOMMEND), sourcing details, and "sizes unknown".
- New tables:
  - `configuration_placements`: position, size, method, text or custom description
  - `configuration_sizes` (TECH-01)
  - `configuration_personalization_rows` (TECH-01)
  - `configuration_files` (TECH-01): role ARTWORK, LOGO, REFERENCE or GARMENT_PHOTO, plus the customer's low-resolution choice
- New artwork choices LOGO and REFERENCE (COM-FR-050).

### Rules (`apps/api/src/configuration/garment.ts`, pure and tested)
- **Placement conflicts** (COM-FR-026):
  - overlapping areas (full front with any chest position, full back with upper back, a large centre design with a side chest)
  - embroidery across a whole front or back (too heavy)
  - large designs on sleeves
  - a custom position without a description
  - Each conflict comes with fixes that change only the placement involved ("Remove left chest", "Print this area instead", "Make it standard size"). Placements are saved even when they conflict, so fixing one never resets anything else.
- **Artwork resolution** (COM-FR-051): design widths are small 7 cm, standard 10 cm and large 28 cm. At the Stage 1.4 raster target of 300 DPI, a raster file narrower than the largest placement needs gets a warning with three choices: replace it, clean it up for me, review it as it is. Vector files (PDF, AI, SVG) never warn. **It never blocks the order.**
- **Personalization CSV** (COM-FR-029):
  - handles quotes, doubled quotes, commas or semicolons, Windows line endings and a BOM
  - the first row names the columns (name, number, department, size), in any order, and any subset
  - checks each row: name up to 30 characters, number 1–3 digits, department up to 40, size from XS–4XL (XXL and XXXL accepted), each row needs something
  - reports every problem with its line number; up to 5,000 rows

### Pricing
- **Sizes** add up to the quantity. With "I don't know the sizes yet" the total quantity is used and the estimate says so (COM-FR-027).
- The calculator takes **adjustments** from answers outside the option list:
  - customer-supplied → ESTIMATE, "the price is final after we inspect them" (COM-FR-023)
  - make from scratch → QUOTE_REQUIRED (pattern, fabric and sample first)
  - help me choose → QUOTE_REQUIRED with a recommendation
  - decoration "Recommend for me", more than one placement, unknown sizes → estimate with a reason

### API (all draft routes are owner-only; others get 404)
- `PATCH /v1/configurations/:id` also takes sourcing, decoration, details (merged, so earlier answers are kept), sizes, sizes unknown and placements. "Send later" is refused where the product's rule doesn't allow it (COM-FR-050).
- `POST /v1/configurations/:id/files` attaches the customer's own upload; someone else's file is 404, and a rejected file is refused. `POST …/files/:linkId/low-resolution` records replace, clean-up or review; `DELETE …/files/:linkId` removes the file, leaving it unattached for the 7-day clean-up.
- `POST /v1/configurations/:id/personalization` imports a CSV upload (purpose CSV_IMPORT). Nothing is saved until every row passes.
- **Uploads for visitors without an account:** file uploads need a session (TECH-04). When a visitor saves the design with their email, they also get a guest session scoped to that one draft, which they already control through the browser cookie. They can then upload artwork.

### Web: the garment configurator (polo and every GARMENT product; Pen 22 · O2tORo, 19–20)
- **Six steady steps** (COM-FR-010): Product, Customize, Quantity, Artwork, Delivery, Review.
- **Product:**
  - the four sourcing cards
  - Source & Customize shows the garment options (colour, fabric)
  - Make From Scratch asks for reference, fit, fabric, colour, labels, and "Make a sample first"
  - I'll Supply the Garment asks what you're sending and how we receive it, and says the price is final after inspection
  - Help Me Choose asks what it's for
- **Customize:**
  - the decoration cards
  - the **garment map**: an SVG of the front and back where each zone can be tapped or chosen by keyboard (checkbox semantics), plus the same positions as text controls
  - per placement: size, method (for "Both") and text or a custom description
  - **conflict notices with one-click fixes**
- **Quantity:**
  - the XS–4XL size grid with the total
  - "I don't know the sizes yet" with a total quantity, plus the Group Collection suggestion as a placeholder (enabled in T12-01)
  - names, numbers and departments by CSV upload with a row-by-row error list
- **Artwork:**
  - ready artwork, logo, reference, design help, or send later (shown only where allowed)
  - uploads through FileUpload (progress, retry, type checks)
  - each file shows its status and, for low resolution, the replace / clean up / review choice
- **Delivery:** chosen at checkout. **Review:** every answer with an Edit link.
- The **preview panel** stays in view on desktop and is labelled "Preview only, not to scale. You'll approve the final design in a proof before we produce." (COM-FR-053)
- The **estimate** sits beside the choices, with the reasons, autosave state and "Email me a link".
- Autosave comes from a shared `useDraft` hook: one save at a time, drafts created on the first change, retry on failure.

## Differences from the design

1. **Garment image:** Pen's preview uses a photo of the polo with the logo placed on it. There's no garment photography or logo compositing yet, so the preview is a line drawing with highlighted zones. It is still labelled approximate.
2. **Thread colours** (Pen 22: "Matched from your file") need artwork analysis. Not built; staff choose them in the proof.
3. **Step header bar:** Pen shows a numbered step bar with "Draft saved / Save & exit" at the top. The page uses numbered sections that expand as you go (the T03-03 pattern, COM-FR-040), with the save state in the estimate panel.
4. **File types:** COM-FR-054 lists JPEG, PNG and PDF. The owner has since approved AI and SVG (TECH-04 amended in T01-05), so the artwork upload accepts PDF, AI, SVG, PNG and JPEG. **PRD-02 COM-FR-054 should be updated** to match.

## Questions / conflicts found

- **COM-FR-054 against the owner's decision:** see difference 4. It's recorded in ATTENTION for a PRD update.
- **Placement prices:** extra placements keep the price an estimate ("confirmed in the final price"). Real per-placement prices need rules per decoration method and size, a later pricing update. **Needed:** per-placement embroidery and print prices.
- **Design print widths:** 7, 10 and 28 cm for small, standard and large are working values for the resolution check. **Confirm with production.**
- **Visitor uploads:** a visitor's first upload needs an email (it starts a guest session). This keeps uploads tied to a person, as TECH-04 requires.
