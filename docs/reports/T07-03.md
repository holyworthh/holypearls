## Plan as built
1. **Order page, actions first (FUL-FR-010–011).** The page runs in this order:
   1. Needs you, shown only when true: proofs awaiting approval, a payment due now, and holds that need the customer.
   2. Where each item stands: item, proof, production, status.
   3. Timeline: a few real milestones.
   4. Payment.
   5. Shipments.
   6. Records and support.
   7. A separate activity history that can't be edited (FUL-FR-040).
2. **Find my order (FUL-FR-033).** The customer enters the order number and the checkout email. A match emails the order's secure link to that address; the screen shows only a masked address. No match gives one message that doesn't say which detail was wrong. Requests are rate-limited (guest-link verify class).
3. **Cancellation (FUL-FR-050–051).**
   - Customers can cancel online only before production, meaning every item is still waiting or queued, and while no payment is being processed.
   - After that, the page shows "Cancellation isn't available" and a link to contact support with the order filled in.
   - Staff can cancel after commitment with a reason and an MFA check in the last 15 minutes (TECH-03 §9), from the production item panel.
   - Cancelling cancels the items (with history), any open holds, unpaid installments and open payments. It records the refund due (what was paid) and emails the customer.
4. **Scope change (FUL-FR-052).** For an order made from a quote, "Change what you ordered" opens a change request (no proof, the stage recorded). The accepted quote version is never edited. Staff see it in Proofs → Change requests.
5. **Refresh rules (PRD-00 §12).** The order page refreshes when the tab regains focus. It checks at most every 30 seconds while the tab is visible, and only for the first 10 minutes on the page.

## What was built
- **Data (migration `0034_order_cancellation`):** `orders.cancelled_at`, `cancelled_by` (CUSTOMER or STAFF), `cancel_reason` and `refund_due_minor`.
- **API:**
  - `POST /v1/orders/find`;
  - `POST /v1/orders/:id/cancel`;
  - `POST /v1/admin/production/orders/:id/cancel` (PRODUCTION_MANAGE + recent MFA);
  - change requests accept no `proofId` for scope changes;
  - the order view gains `cancellation`, `cancel` (allowed, blockedBy, refundMinor), `canChangeScope` and `activity`.
  - **Activity history:** order placed, payments, proof decisions, item milestones, holds, change requests and cancellation. QC notes and internal notes are never included (FUL-FR-016).
- **Emails:** `order_link` (Find my order) and `order_cancelled` (refund wording). Both carry the order link.
- **Web:**
  - `/orders/[id]` rebuilt per Pen 51/27;
  - `/orders/[id]/cancel` (allowed and blocked);
  - `/orders/[id]/change` (scope change and "sent");
  - `/track` (form, link sent, not found, rate limited);
  - an expired link now offers "Track with order number" (Pen 53 D);
  - header and footer "Track an order" go to `/track`;
  - `OrderRefresh`.
- **Admin:** the production item panel has "Cancel order" with a reason, and the step-up dialog when MFA is stale.
- **Tests:** `src/testing/flows.ts` holds shared customer and staff order flows.

## Tests (`orders/tracking.test.ts`, 6, all pass locally)
- **Find my order:**
  - a wrong email or number gets the same 404 message;
  - a bad form gets 400;
  - a match (case-insensitive) returns a masked address and never opens the order by itself;
  - the emailed link opens the order.
- **Order data:**
  - activity is newest first;
  - a QC rework appears as "went back into production after quality check", and its internal note never appears;
  - cancel is allowed before production and blocked (STARTED) after.
- **Customer cancel:**
  - 404 for another customer and for a stranger;
  - the owner cancels: every item is CANCELLED, the summary is CANCELLED, the refund due equals what was paid, the email is queued and the activity entry is written;
  - a second cancel gets 409.
- **Blocked after production:** 422 with the Pen message, and the item is unchanged.
- **Scope change:**
  - an OPEN change request with no proof;
  - a second one is refused while the first is open;
  - **the accepted quote version and its lines are byte-for-byte unchanged**;
  - refused for a paid checkout order (NOT_FROM_QUOTE).
- **Staff cancel:**
  - 403 without PRODUCTION_MANAGE;
  - 400 with no reason;
  - **RECENT_MFA_REQUIRED with a 16-minute-old check, and nothing changes**;
  - with fresh MFA: the deposit is recorded as the refund due, the unpaid balance is CANCELLED, it's audited, and a repeat gets 409.

## Questions and conflicts
1. **Cancellation policy.** No document defines one. TECH-01 §4 allows a customer cancel "before production if policy allows", so the policy as built is: online cancel while every item is waiting or queued. Owner to confirm, or to name products that can't be cancelled once ordered (ATTENTION 31).
2. **Refunds.** Cancelling records `refund_due_minor` and tells the customer finance will refund them. Issuing refunds (Paystack refund API, REFUND_ISSUE, MFA) is T13-05. Until then, finance refunds by hand in Paystack (ATTENTION 31).
3. **"Request cancellation" after production (FUL-FR-051)** is the contact form with the order filled in (Pen 127 C6TAE), not a separate record.
4. **Find by phone** (Pen 53) isn't built: phone numbers aren't verified, and FUL-FR-033 names email.
5. **Local dev note.** On localhost, cookies are shared across ports, so a staff session from the admin (port 3002) makes the web app act as that staff user and hides guest links. This doesn't happen in production, where the admin has its own domain.
