## What was built

- **Branch:** `feature/t11-01c-template-orders`. **PR:** see the tracker row.
- **Migration `0044_template_orders.sql`:**
  - **`template_orders`:** the template, organization, location, the new configuration (unique), who ordered, the route (`CHECKOUT` or `QUOTE`), and the saved copy (`snapshot`: name, version, fields, assets, the buyer's choices, quantity, location and time). Composite foreign keys keep the template and the location inside the organization.
  - **`configurations.controls`:** set on a configuration made from a template. It holds the template, organization, location, name and the list of editable field keys.
- **Configuration module:**
  - **`copies.ts`:** `insertConfigurationCopy`, the copy step reorder already used, now shared by reorder and template orders. Reorder's behaviour is unchanged.
  - **`configurationFromTemplate`:** copies the template's saved configuration for the buyer, applies their choices (checked against today's options), sets the quantity, and prices it. If the quantity changed, the original's size split is dropped.
  - **`previewTemplatePrice`:** today's price for the buyer's choices, for the price panel. It saves no design.
  - **`refuseLockedChange`:** every draft change on a template design (`PATCH /v1/configurations/:id`, adding or removing artwork files, low-resolution actions, personalisation imports) is refused with `LOCKED_FIELD` unless the template marks that field editable.
  - **Configuration view:** gains `fromTemplate`. The cart's Edit link for a template design goes back to the template order page.
  - **`quoteIn`:** can record where the quote is for (organization and location).
- **Sales:** a checkout whose items all come from the same template location starts placed for that location (frame 131 "Deliver to"), if the buyer may still order for it. The buyer can change it as usual.
- **Organizations module (`template-orders.ts`):**
  - **`GET …/templates/:id/order`:**
    - The template.
    - The editable choices, with today's options.
    - Whether the quantity is editable.
    - The allowed locations: the template's locations that are also the buyer's.
    - Why it's blocked, if it is: retired asset, not approved, no permission, or no location.
  - **`POST …/templates/:id/price`:** the price preview. It refuses locked fields too.
  - **`POST …/templates/:id/orders`** (idempotent):
    - Checks `CREATE_REQUESTS`, the template version, APPROVED status, the location and every changed field.
    - Makes the configuration and puts it in the cart (PRICE_READY), or sends a quote request for the location using the buyer's account details.
    - Records the template order and an audit entry, `template.ordered`.
  - **OpenAPI:** the 3 routes are documented.
- **Customer site:**
  - **`/workspace/[id]/templates/[templateId]/order` (frame 131):**
    - The eyebrow with the template and asset versions.
    - "Set by your company (locked)" with lock icons.
    - "Your choices": editable options with their reasons, quantity, and Deliver to with "Only your allowed locations are listed".
    - A price panel with PRICE READY or QUOTE REQUIRED, the total with VAT, and the button: "Go to checkout" (adds to the cart and opens checkout) or "Request a quote" (shows the quote reference).
    - The saved-copy and not-an-exact-reorder notes.
    - The blocked state (giRIE).
  - **The template detail** gains "Order from template" for approved templates and people who can place orders.

## Permission rules added

- **Ordering:** `CREATE_REQUESTS` (Owner, Admin, Location Manager, Buyer). A Viewer or Approver gets 403, and the order form says why.
- **Templates:** the template must be visible to the member (their locations; 404 otherwise) and APPROVED. NEEDS_REVIEW is `TEMPLATE_BLOCKED`; anything else is `TEMPLATE_NOT_APPROVED`.
- **Location:** one of the template's locations that is also the member's. Otherwise `LOCATION_NOT_ALLOWED` (403).
- **Locked fields:** refused with `LOCKED_FIELD` (422) on the order, the price preview and every later draft change. An unknown field is 400.
- **Stale template:** an older template version is refused with 409, so nobody orders a superseded specification.

## Differences from the design or PRD, and why

1. **Size selector (frame 131 desktop):** sizes aren't asked on the order page. A garment template's size split is kept when the quantity is unchanged. A new quantity drops it, and sizes are given on the design (the size field is editable when the template says so) or collected by a group (T12). The price uses the quantity, as the configurator does.
2. **"Approval needed" (giRIE):** internal approval and order requests are T11-04. Today a template order goes straight to checkout or a quote.
3. **"Go to checkout"** adds the item to the cart and opens checkout. Other ready items already in the cart are checked out with it, as from the cart.
4. **"Order" on the index cards (frame 68):** cards stay a single link to the detail, where "Order from template" is. A button inside a link isn't valid HTML.
5. **Quote confirmation:** the order page shows the quote reference and links to the account's quotes. Configuration quotes don't appear in the workspace Projects list yet (T10-03 lists projects).

## Questions / conflicts found

1. **Where a template order's quote shows in the workspace:** quotes from configurations now carry the organization and location, but the workspace lists (T10-03) show projects and orders. Suggested follow-up: list placed configuration quotes in workspace Projects or Orders.
2. **Product rule changes:** as noted in T11-01b, a template isn't moved to NEEDS_REVIEW when a product rule changes. Ordering always uses today's options and prices, so an option the product dropped is refused as a validation error.
