# T03-08 — Other product families, timing, delivery and Request Final Quote

Status: DONE (pending CI and merge).

Commits / PR: branch `feature/t03-08-product-families-timing-quote-request`. The pull request is linked in `docs/tracker/TICKETS.csv`.

## What was built

### Data
- **Migration `0015_quotes`:** a minimal `quotes` table: reference, status (TECH-01 §4 quote statuses; new quotes are PENDING_REVIEW), customer, configuration, `request_snapshot` (a copy of every answer and the price shown, TECH-01 §5), contact (name, phone, note) and version. T05-03 builds quote versions on top of it.
- **Migration `0016_quote_reference_sequence`:** `quote_number_seq`, formatted as `HP-Q-2026-0031`. References are for people only and never grant access (TECH-01 §1).
- **Family and delivery answers** are stored in the draft's `details`, merged on every save, so earlier answers are kept. There's no new column.

### Rules (`apps/api/src/configuration/families.ts`, pure and tested)
- **Large format and wall art (COM-FR-045):**
  - standard or custom size; custom sizes take a width and height in cm, m or inches, are stored in cm, and show the area in m²
  - print only, delivered, or installation required; installation asks for site, surface, height, access and date
  - a custom size or installation moves the price to **QUOTE_REQUIRED**
  - installation is its own choice, never folded into delivery
- **Packaging (COM-FR-046):** standard structure, or a custom structure (dimensions, use, material, closure, handles, insert, "make a sample first"), which moves to **QUOTE_REQUIRED**.
- **Promotional (COM-FR-047):** the catalogue item, or "Source something similar", which moves to **QUOTE_REQUIRED** with "not promised until confirmed" wording.
- **Embroidery and apparel print:** on our garments, or on the customer's own, which is **final after inspection** (ESTIMATE_READY, the same rule as T03-05).
- **Destinations (COM-FR-072):** Lagos, elsewhere in Nigeria, or outside Nigeria. An international address continues through the configuration as **QUOTE_REQUIRED**, "freight is confirmed in your quote".
- **Timing (COM-FR-070, 071):**
  - production days (standard, rush or priority, the same maths as the calculator) and transit days are returned separately
  - a "needed by" date is checked in working days
  - the result is on time, "Rush should arrive by…" (with a one-click switch), or **needs production review**, which keeps the price an estimate (ESTIMATE_READY, `DEADLINE_REVIEW`)
- **Switching to a quote never clears anything (COM-FR-060).** Adjustments only change the price state and reasons; choices, quantity and details stay.

### API
- `GET/PATCH /v1/configurations/:id` now returns:
  - `family`
  - `details` (all family and delivery answers)
  - `timing`: production, transit, whether transit is quoted, expected date, verdict, suggested rush and message
  - `quote`: reference and status, once requested
- `PATCH` accepts the new detail fields (validated by `detailsSchema`) and `rush`.
- **`POST /v1/configurations/:id/quote-request`** (COM-FR-061), owner only, others get 404; rate-limited as CONTACT:
  - takes a name, email, optional phone and note
  - refuses a design with no quantity (400) or with a blocking reason (422, for example below the minimum)
  - otherwise, in one transaction:
    - links the customer
    - creates the PENDING_REVIEW quote with the snapshot
    - moves the design to QUOTE_REQUESTED, so it can't change under the staff pricing it
    - emails a secure link back to the design with the reference
  - asking again returns the same reference

### Web
- A shared `FamilyDelivery.tsx` provides `FamilyFields`, `DeliveryFields` and `QuoteRequest`.
- **Inline configurator (all non-garment products):**
  - the family questions sit under Options
  - the Delivery step now has the destination, city and country, "Needed by" and the timing choice
  - the timing panel shows "Production: x–y working days after proof approval" and "Delivery: a–b working days" (or "freight … confirmed in your quote") separately, with the deadline message
- **Garment configurator:** the same delivery, timing and quote request.
- **Button wording (COM-FR-061):**
  - quote-led and estimate states end with **Request Final Quote**, which opens a short form
  - after sending, the page shows "Quote requested · HP-Q-…"
  - PRICE_READY still shows "Continue to checkout" (disabled until T04-01)

## Differences from the design

1. **Custom size pricing:** Pen 38 (PDP family state board, "CUSTOM SIZE") says custom sizes are "calculated by area; very large sizes move to a quote". There are no per-area price rules yet (T03-01 prices per unit with size add-ons), so under COM-FR-060 every custom size is QUOTE_REQUIRED for now. **Needed:** per-m² prices and a "very large" threshold per material to price by area automatically.
2. **Roll-up "Banner + stand / Replacement print" route and base size (Pen 25):** the seed product uses the three sizes from Stage 1.4 test data. Pen notes the 85 × 200 cm size was unsourced. The route and base options are catalogue data (admin) rather than code, and are left to the catalogue owner.
3. **Custom packaging (Pen 30)** is a short brief with a reference upload and budget range rather than a configurator. Here the structure fields sit inside the configurator, with a "make a sample first" tick; reference files use the Artwork step. A budget field isn't in COM-FR-046, so it's not added.
4. **Installation photos (COM-FR-045):** asked for as reference files in the Artwork step (existing uploads), not a separate uploader.
5. **Timing presentation:** Pen has no dedicated timing panel frame; it uses the existing `placeholder`-style panel and warning tint tokens.

## Questions / conflicts found

- **Pen 38 vs COM-FR-060 on custom sizes:** see difference 1. The PRD is followed.
- **Transit days** are working values until delivery zones exist (T04-01): Lagos 1–2, elsewhere in Nigeria 2–5 working days. **Confirm with operations.**
- **Deadline review:** a date we can't meet even with priority rush keeps the price as an estimate with "we'll review it with you". Is that the intended "needs production review" timing (COM-FR-071), or should it block checkout until staff confirm?
- **Quote link:** the quote page doesn't exist until T05. The email links back to the design (now read-only, showing the quote reference). T05-03 should switch the link purpose to QUOTE.
