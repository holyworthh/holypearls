## What was built

### Data (migration `0028_projects`, TECH-01 §2 names)
- **`projects`**:
  - type and status (TECH-01 §4);
  - the step the customer was on;
  - every answer, kept as JSON against the question keys in `@holypearls/contracts`;
  - the owner: the account, the browser's device key (hashed) or the guest link;
  - a version and the last activity time.
- **The `HP-P-…` reference** stays empty until submit (T05-03).
- **`project_recommendations`**:
  - one row per suggestion, keyed by the rule that made it;
  - the suggested quantity and the customer's own quantity;
  - SUGGESTED, INCLUDED or REMOVED;
  - `customer_overridden`, and whether the brief still suggests it.
- **`project_items`**: items the customer added, either catalogue products or custom items, with a loose "about 30" note.
- **`project_files`**: brand files, and reference files for custom items.
- **`recommendation_rules`** and **`recommendation_rule_sets`**: the rules per project type, with a version per type.
- **Draft expiry:** a draft untouched for 90 days becomes EXPIRED in the daily clean-up, and its files are let go, the same as configuration drafts.

### How suggestions work (PROJ-FR-003–004)
- **Rules:**
  - Each rule suggests a catalogue product or a described item ("Main backdrop") in Recommended, Useful additions or Optional, with the reason the customer sees.
  - **When it applies:** always, when a number answer passes a value, or when an answer is chosen.
  - **How many:** a fixed number, so many per answer, or staff confirm after review.
- **Recommended items** start in the project; useful additions and optional extras are offered.
- **The customer's edits win:**
  - Anything the customer touches (add, remove, change quantity) is marked theirs.
  - A later change to the brief refreshes the suggestion but never undoes the choice or the quantity.
  - A suggestion that stops applying is dropped only if the customer never touched it. Otherwise it stays, labelled "No longer suggested for your brief".
  - The customer's own items are never changed.
- **Staff rule edits:** saving keeps each rule's id, so customers' choices survive. Customers see a change the next time they save their project.
- **Starting rules** for Event branding and Staff uniforms come from Pen 110 and Stage 3.1D §18–19.
  - They're loaded by a new seed step, only where a type has no rules yet.
  - They are starting values for staff to edit, not business promises (PRD-00 §17).

### Customer pages (web)
- **`/start` (Pen 23):**
  - "What are we making together?" with "Help me figure it out" (two questions that suggest project types) and "In your own words".
  - The six common orders as whole-card buttons, then the other seven types under **More kinds of project**.
  - The "Something else?" band and "How a project works".
  - A returning visitor sees "Continue your Event branding project — Recommendations".
  - The page is indexable.
- **Builder `/start/[id]/[step]`** (noindex, private):
  - **The six steps:** Project, Scale, Date & location, Brand, Recommendations and Review.
  - **Questions by type** (Stage 3.1D §6–9), with "I'm not sure yet" on numbers, dates, yes/no and choices.
  - **Autosave:** about 0.7 s after typing, with "Saving…", "Saved just now", and "Not saved. Try again".
  - **Saves from two tabs** load the latest version with a notice instead of overwriting.
  - **Recommendations (Pen 24):**
    - the brief as chips, with Edit brief;
    - suggestion cards with the reason, "From ₦…", a quantity stepper, In project / Remove / Undo / Add;
    - optional extras as rows;
    - **Your own items**, with catalogue search or a custom item plus a reference file.
  - **The summary panel** shows the items, open items (sizes later, artwork later, date to be confirmed…), "Price · QUOTE REQUIRED", and **Need help planning this?** (the help inbox with the project and its files).
  - **Review:** every answer with Edit links, and the items.
  - **Save and resume (PROJ-FR-007):** **Email me a link** sends a guest link (purpose PROJECT) that opens the step the customer was on. Signing in makes the browser's drafts the account's.
- **Guest uploads:** uploads need a session (TECH-04), so a guest saves the link first and then uploads. Signed-in customers upload straight away.
- **"Start a Project" buttons** across the site now go to `/start`: the header, footer, homepage, Projects, About and Search. The "Tell us about your project" links still go to the contact form.

### Admin (apps/admin, ADR-017)
- **`/projects/rules` (Pen 110):**
  - pick a project type;
  - the rules table: group, suggest, when, quantity, reason;
  - the edit panel: item, product or described item, group, when, quantity, reason, active, move up or down, delete;
  - **Preview with N guests**;
  - **Save rules**, with a version check and audit entry `recommendation_rules.saved` (QUOTE_MANAGE);
  - states: loading, empty, error, 403, saved, and "needs fixing" on rules the API refuses.
- **`/projects`** says the queue arrives with T05-03 and links to the rules.

### Permissions
- **Projects:** the owner (account, browser or link) opens a project; everyone else gets 404, staff on the customer site included.
- **Recommendation rules:** QUOTE_MANAGE only. Staff without it get 403; customers get 404 (admin routes only work through the admin app).
- **Project files:** the project's owner, its link's guest, and QUOTE_MANAGE staff.
- **Test admin Ada** gains QUOTE_MANAGE; the identity test is updated to match.

## Questions and conflicts

1. **Draft reference:** Pen 24 shows `HP-P-2026-0142 · DRAFT` on a draft. PRD-03 PROJ-FR-011 gives the reference when the project is submitted. I followed the PRD; drafts show "DRAFT · SAVED AS YOU GO".
2. **Step count:** Pen 124 labels the review "Step 4 of 4". The PRD and Stage 3.1D fix six steps, so it reads "Step 6 of 6" (T05-03 builds that screen).
3. **Timing guidance:** "Comfortable" (6+ weeks), "Tight" (3–6) and "Production review needed" (under 3) are starting values in code. They are guidance only and never promise a date. Suggestion: make them an admin setting with the lead times (PRD-00 §17), perhaps in T13-01.
4. **Where staff rule changes show:** they reach existing drafts the next time the customer saves, not instantly, so a page that's already open doesn't change while someone reads it.
5. **Project pages:** the public project pages (`/projects/[slug]`) still link to the contact form. A suggestion for later: "Start this project" could open `/start` with that type already chosen.
