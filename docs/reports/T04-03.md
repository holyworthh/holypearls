## What was built

### Data (migration `0026_payments`)
- **`payment_settings`**: one row, with online payments **off**, the channels card, bank transfer and USSD, international cards off and USD off. It also holds the default schedule. Staff edit it in T04-05.
- **`payment_schedules`** → **`installments`** → **`payment_intents`** → **`payment_attempts`**, with the TECH-01 §4 statuses as enums.
  - A checkout's schedule is one "Pay in full" installment.
  - Quotes add deposit and balance schedules in T05-05. The schedule's source check is ready for that.
- **One success per intent:** `payment_intents.succeeded_attempt_id` is unique, and a check constraint requires it whenever the intent is SUCCEEDED. Successes are applied with the attempt and intent rows locked (TECH-05 §7).
- **`payment_attempts`** keeps:
  - the `HPPAY-<uuid>` reference (unique);
  - the amount asked for, and the amount, currency, channel and wording Paystack reported;
  - every check (when, from where, what Paystack said; PAY-FR-010).
- **`paystack_events`** stores each webhook exactly as received. It is unique on the event plus Paystack's transaction ID, so a resend saves nothing new.
- **`payment_reviews`**: reasons UNCLEAR_RESULT, AMOUNT_MISMATCH, DUPLICATE_PAYMENT and PAID_PARTICIPANT_REMOVED. Only one open case per attempt and reason.
- **`configuration_status` gains `ORDERED`** for a paid design. This is the only change to an existing type.

### Provider (`packages/payments`, new)
- **`PaymentGateway` interface:** initialize, verify, and webhook signature check.
- **`PaystackGateway`:**
  - Initialize sends the server's amount in kobo, the reference, email, enabled channels, the callback URL and metadata IDs.
  - Verify maps Paystack's status words. Anything not final counts as pending, and an unknown reference is reported as `not_found`.
  - Webhooks are checked with HMAC SHA-512 over the raw body.
- **`FakeGateway`** is in `packages/testkit`. Tests decide what Paystack says, and it signs webhooks the same way Paystack does.

### Rules (`apps/api/src/payments/payments.ts`)
- **Pay** (`POST /v1/checkouts/:id/payments`; Idempotency-Key; COMMERCIAL_WRITE limit). The amount always comes from the checkout, never from the request.
  - The intent is marked PROCESSING before Paystack is called. A second key gets 409 `PAYMENT_IN_PROGRESS` with the time a new attempt may start (10 minutes, TECH-05 §6).
  - When Paystack refuses to start, the attempt is FAILED and the intent goes back to OPEN. The customer sees "We couldn't start the payment. Try again."
  - When it starts, the attempt becomes PENDING and its seven checks are queued in the same transaction: 2 min, 10 min, 30 min, 2 h, 6 h, 24 h and 48 h.
- **Webhook** (`POST /v1/webhooks/paystack`; raw body kept by a small middleware before the JSON parser): a wrong or missing signature gets 403 and nothing is saved. A first-time `charge.*` event queues `payment.verify`.
- **Applying a result** (`applyResult`) works from Paystack's verify API only, never from the webhook's own words. It is safe to repeat and to run in parallel.
  - **Success with the right amount and currency:** the attempt and intent are SUCCEEDED and the installment PAID. The checkout becomes COMPLETED, its designs become ORDERED and leave the cart, and the change is audited. T04-05 adds the order, receipt and email at this point (`onIntentSucceeded`).
  - **Wrong amount or currency:** the attempt is SUCCEEDED (money was taken) and the intent goes to REVIEW_REQUIRED with an AMOUNT_MISMATCH review. Nothing is credited.
  - **A second success:** both attempts are kept and the checkout completes once. The intent goes to REVIEW_REQUIRED with a DUPLICATE_PAYMENT review.
  - **Failed or abandoned:** the attempt becomes FAILED or ABANDONED, and the intent goes back to OPEN unless another attempt is still running.
  - **No final answer at 48 hours:** the attempt is UNKNOWN, the intent REVIEW_REQUIRED, and an UNCLEAR_RESULT review opens.
- **Customer-facing status** (`status.ts`) is worked out in the TECH-01 §4 order, never stored.
- **A checkout with a payment under way is protected.** Its answers can't change (409 `PAYMENT_IN_PROGRESS`). "Check out" returns that same checkout. Editing or removing its designs is refused. The daily clean-up doesn't expire it.
- When a checkout closes, its unpaid intent is cancelled.

### Worker
- `payment.verify` and `payment.check` call the same services as the API.
- Their dead letters are logged and reported. The 48-hour check still settles the attempt.

### Web
- **Checkout review (Pen 43/44):**
  - Pay starts the attempt, then opens Paystack's popup (`js.paystack.co/v2/inline.js`). If the popup can't open, it goes to Paystack's hosted page.
  - One Idempotency-Key per press.
  - Notices for "isn't open yet", "paused", "didn't go through" and "already in progress", with "Start a new payment" after 10 minutes.
  - "We accept" lists only the channels switched on (PAY-FR-007).
- **Processing page** `/checkout/[id]/processing` (Pen 45 B, C and D):
  - It asks the server every 3 s, then every 15 s after two minutes. It never shows paid or failed before the server knows.
  - Its states are: confirming; didn't go through (with "Still saved", Try again, and the reference for support); we're checking (review); and payment received (with the next steps).
  - Focus moves to the result heading.
- **Security headers:**
  - The CSP allows Paystack's script, styles, API and checkout frame.
  - `Permissions-Policy` lets Paystack's frame use the Payment Request API (Apple Pay).

### Config and infrastructure
- `PAYSTACK_SECRET_KEY` is read by the api and worker. Production accepts only `sk_live_` keys, and other environments only `sk_test_`. Without a key, payments show as not available.
- Staging Render has the test keys (2026-10-01).
- The dev database is migrated and has online payments on, for local testing.

## Questions / conflicts found

1. **Pen 45 shows an order number while processing and after a failure.** TECH-05 §8 (higher source) creates the order only when the payment succeeds. Followed TECH-05: those screens show the amount and payment reference. T04-05 adds the order number to the success screen.
2. **Pen 45 C has "Use another payment method".** PAY-FR-007 has the customer choose the method inside Paystack, so a second button would do the same as Try again. Built "Back to checkout" instead.
3. **Pen 45 D "Order confirmed".** Until T04-05 creates orders, the success screen says "Payment received".
4. **Receipts table:** moved to T04-05's migration, with the orders it references.
5. **TECH-02 §1 names `POST /v1/payments/intents/:id/attempts`.** Checkouts pay through `POST /v1/checkouts/:id/payments`, because a checkout's intent is created on the first press. The intents route arrives with quote installments (T05-05).
6. **"Abandoned" in the first 30 minutes:** see ATTENTION, "T04-03, closed popup".
7. **A success after the checkout closed or a review opened** is recorded and sent to staff as UNCLEAR_RESULT, not credited automatically (TECH-01 §4: REVIEW_REQUIRED leaves only by staff decision).
