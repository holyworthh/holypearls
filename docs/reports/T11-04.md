## What was built

- **Branch:** `feature/t11-04-order-approvals`. **PR:** https://github.com/holyworthh/holypearls/pull/96.
- **Migration `0045_order_approvals.sql`:**
  - **`approval_policies`:** one row per organization. The rule (NONE, ALL, ABOVE_AMOUNT, REQUESTERS), the amount, the currency, and "separate requester and approver" (on by default).
  - **`approval_policy_requesters`:** the members whose requests need approval under REQUESTERS.
  - **`order_requests`:**
    - The organization and location (composite foreign key), title, status and requester.
    - The estimate, the held designs, and the saved copy of what was asked.
    - The quote it became, submission and send times, and `version`.
  - **`organization_approvals`:** decision (APPROVED, REJECTED, CHANGES_REQUESTED), approved amount, scope, note and approver. A trigger refuses any UPDATE or DELETE, so approvals can't be edited by any route.
  - **New `configuration_status` AWAITING_APPROVAL:** a design held while it waits.
  - `template_orders.route` gains APPROVAL.
- **Contracts (`approvals.ts`):** rules and labels, the policy and its update, request statuses, the list, the detail with approvals and history, and the decision input. The template order form gains `approval`, and its result gains the APPROVAL route.
- **API:**
  - **`organizations/approvals.ts`:**
    - The policy: everyone reads it; only an Owner sets it.
    - `approvalNeeded`: above an amount, a request with no price yet needs approval too.
    - Creating an order request: designs held, approvers for the location emailed.
    - List and detail, scoped (see Permission rules).
    - Decide: approve, request changes or decline.
      - Approving records the approved amount (the estimate, or an amount the approver enters) and the scope.
      - In the same transaction, it sends the held designs to HolyPearls as a quote request owned by the requester and placed for the location. The request becomes SENT_TO_HOLYPEARLS.
      - Request changes (DRAFT) and decline (REJECTED) need a note and give the designs back.
    - Resubmit at today's price, and cancel. Both are for the requester only.
    - Every step is audited.
  - **Template orders (T11-01c):** when the rule applies, the order becomes an order request instead of going to checkout or a quote (frame 131 "this becomes an order request for approval").
  - **Configuration module:**
    - Hold and release designs for approval.
    - `heldQuotable`: checks held designs can be quoted before anything changes.
    - `currentDesigns`: today's price, for resubmitting.
    - `quoteIn` can quote held designs for an owner other than the person acting: the approver sends the requester's request.
  - **Email:**
    - `order_request_submitted` to the approvers for the location.
    - `order_request_decided` to the requester.
  - **OpenAPI:** the 7 routes are documented.
- **Customer site:**
  - **"Approvals" in the sidebar** (and on the 390 px More page): "Awaiting your approval" first for approvers, then the other requests they can see.
  - **`/workspace/[id]/approvals/[requestId]` (frames 72, 73A):**
    - The status chip, title, and requested by, location, date and estimated total.
    - "What's being ordered", with template items marked "From approved template", and History.
    - **"Your decision":** the policy line ("ACME policy: orders above ₦250,000 need an approver. This one is ₦… over."), Approve ₦…, Request changes, Decline, the note, and the frame's footer line.
    - Requesters get Cancel request, and Send for approval again after changes.
    - With separation on, an approver who made the request sees why they can't decide.
  - **"Approval rule" tab under Team & Settings:** the Owner chooses the rule, the amount or people, and separation. Everyone else reads it.
  - **Template order page:** "Approval needed" and "Send for approval" when the rule applies.

## Permission rules added

- **Approval rule:** only the Owner sets it. An Admin gets 403 `OWNER_REQUIRED`. Every member can read it.
- **Deciding:**
  - Needs `APPROVE_REQUESTS` and the request's location (TECH-03 §7). A Buyer, Viewer or Location Manager gets 403.
  - With separation on, nobody decides their own request, not even an Owner: 403 `SELF_APPROVAL`.
- **Seeing requests:** the requester sees their own. Approvers and members with `VIEW_RECORDS` see those for their locations. Buyers see only their own. Others get 404.
- **Cancel and resubmit:** the requester only. An approver gets 403 `REQUESTER_ONLY`.
- **Approvals:** insert-only. There is no edit route, and the database refuses changes.

## Differences from the design or PRD, and why

1. **"Needed by" (frame 72):** order requests don't capture a needed-by date yet; template orders don't ask for one. The meta row shows the submission date instead.
2. **"Last approved reorder" history (frame 72):** history shows this request's own events: submitted, decisions, and sent to HolyPearls.
3. **Item thumbnails (frame 72):** the item list shows the product, source, quantity, location and price, without photos.
4. **"Approvals" sidebar item:** frame 72's breadcrumb reads "Approvals", but the sidebar shown has no Approvals item. Approvers need a place to find waiting requests, so Approvals is in the sidebar, with Projects.
5. **Mobile pinned decisions (frame 73A):** on 390 px the decision panel follows the request in one column rather than staying pinned. A sticky action bar can follow.
6. **Order requests come from template orders only.** A cart or project doesn't become an order request yet. Group collections (T12-01) are the next source.

## Questions / conflicts found

1. **Request changes vs TECH-01 §4:** TECH-01 lists PENDING_APPROVAL → APPROVED | REJECTED only. Frame 72 has "Request changes" and mobile frame 73 has "Ask for changes". Following the design (higher source): PENDING_APPROVAL → DRAFT with a CHANGES_REQUESTED approval record, and the requester resubmits (DRAFT → PENDING_APPROVAL). **Proposed TECH-01 §4 amendment:** "PENDING_APPROVAL → DRAFT (changes requested; a CHANGES_REQUESTED approval record is kept)".
2. **APPROVED → SENT_TO_HOLYPEARLS happens in the same transaction:** frame 72 says "Approving sends the request to HolyPearls for a quote". APPROVED is never seen on its own; the approval record keeps the amount and scope.
3. **Who sets the approval rule:** TECH-03 §7 has no row for it. Built as Owner only, because it decides who can spend without a second approver. **Owner to confirm.**
4. **Approve without a price:** above an amount, a request with no price yet needs approval, and the approver enters the amount they approve. HolyPearls' quote is then checked against it in T11-04b.
