## What was built

- **Branch:** `feature/t12-06-company-store`. **PR:** https://github.com/holyworthh/holypearls/pull/104.
- **Migration `0051_company_stores.sql`:**
  - **`company_stores`:** organization, name, status (DRAFT, ACTIVE, PAUSED, ARCHIVED), mode (PROCUREMENT, SELF_ORDER), routing (INDIVIDUAL, COLLECTION), who made it, `version`.
  - **`company_store_items`:** the store's approved templates in order and, with COLLECTION routing, the group collection each item's picks join.
- **Contracts (`groups.ts`):**
  - The store list, the store as the person can use it, setup, procurement order and self-order pick inputs, and the order result.
  - Each item carries its locked description, price for one, the reason it's unavailable, the fields the employee fills in, sizes, colours, branches, and the button and note in outcome words.
  - `collectionSummarySchema` gains `templateId`, so setup can offer the collections made from each template.
- **API (`groups/stores.ts`):**
  - **Setup (MANAGE_STORES):**
    - Create a DRAFT with a name and mode, then set name, mode, routing and items.
    - Items must be approved templates. An item already in the store can stay while its template is under review; it shows as unavailable.
    - With COLLECTION routing, each item names a collection made from that template that isn't cancelled.
    - Procurement stores can't use COLLECTION routing.
    - Opening needs an item (and each collection item its collection). Then pause, reopen and archive. Every change is audited.
  - **Members:**
    - They see ACTIVE stores. A PAUSED store still shows, with "This store is paused". DRAFT and ARCHIVED stores are 404 for them.
    - Items are filtered to the member's locations (frame 78 A: "only approved products for their branch").
    - Routing and collection links are only shown to people who manage stores (B2B-FR-082).
  - **Procurement order (frame 77):**
    - Several items with quantities for one of the buyer's locations. Every line is checked before anything is made.
    - The lines then go together: one order request over the organization's approval rule (T11-04), the cart when every line has a direct price, otherwise one quote request.
    - Each line is kept as a Template Order with its template copy and the store in the snapshot.
  - **Self-order pick (frames 32, 78):** one item with the employee's size, name and branch. The request is the same in both routings.
    - **INDIVIDUAL (J09):** the employee's own one-piece design (their size, their name row), placed for their branch by the same template-order route. It is approval, checkout or quote, and kept as a Template Order.
    - **COLLECTION (J10):** the employee becomes a SUBMITTED participant in the item's collection (origin `STORE:<store name>`) and gets the usual copy by email. Picking again replaces their answers rather than adding a second person. No order is made: the organizer reviews the collection first.
    - The collection's own rules apply: its fields, its branches and its deadline. A closed or paused collection makes the item unavailable.
  - **Shared code:**
    - `organizations/template-orders.ts` now exports `templateLine` (the checks), `placeTemplateLines` (the routing, now for one or more lines) and `approvalPreview`. `orderFromTemplate` uses them unchanged in behaviour.
    - `organizations/templates.ts` exports `templatesFor` (several templates a member can see).
    - `groups/participants.ts` exports `submitFromStore` and `colourChoices`. `submitParticipation` shares the missing-answer check and the email copy.
  - **OpenAPI:** the 6 routes are documented.
- **Customer site:**
  - **"Company Store"** in the workspace navigation (and under More at phone width).
  - **`/workspace/[id]/stores`:** the stores with their status and mode, and "New company store" for people who manage stores.
  - **The store page:**
    - **Procurement (frame 77):** the store header, "Approved products for Lekki" with each item's locked description, price each and a quantity, and "Temporarily unavailable" with its reason.
      - "This order" shows the lines, the estimated total and ACME's approval limit note.
      - The button reads "Send for approval", "Place order" or "Request a quote".
    - **Self-order (frames 32 and 78):** the mode note, "Approved for you", and the item sheet.
      - What ACME set reads as facts in a green "Set by ACME" block, followed by size buttons, the optional name (12 letters) and the branch.
      - The button reads "Request this item" or "Add to ACME team order", with its note. It is sticky above the bottom tabs at phone width.
  - **`/stores/[id]/setup`:** name, who orders, where picks go (self-order only, with "Employees never see or choose this"), the approved templates with a collection per item when needed, and Open, Pause and Archive.

## Permission rules added

- **Setting up, opening, pausing and archiving stores:** `MANAGE_STORES` (Owner and Admin). A Location Manager or Buyer gets 403.
- **Seeing a store:** any active member, for ACTIVE and PAUSED stores, with only the items for their locations. DRAFT and ARCHIVED are 404 for members.
- **Ordering or picking:** needs `CREATE_REQUESTS`. A Viewer gets 403. A procurement location must be one of the buyer's, otherwise 403. An item from another location, or a template not in the store, gives 404.

## Differences from the design or PRD, and why

1. **The allowance in frames 32 and 78 (B, C):** "₦32,500 of ₦75,000 left", "Covered" and "Allowance exceeded" are T12-06b. This ticket shows no allowance.
2. **Frame 77's "Needed by":** procurement orders don't take a needed-by date yet (template orders don't either). The rail shows "Delivery to Lekki".
3. **Frame 78 D "Tell me when it's back":** the unavailable item shows its state and reason. There is no back-in-stock alert: no ticket covers one.
4. **Frame 77's quantity stepper:** a numeric field is used, as on the template order form (T11-01c), so large quantities can be typed.
5. **Collection mode with several items:** frame 32 shows three items joining "the team order". A collection is one design (B2B-FR-051), so each store item links its own collection made from that template.
6. **Individual self-order is one piece:** an employee orders one, in their size. A template whose quantity is locked to more than one shows "Ordered in a fixed quantity by your company" in self-order stores.
7. **Store setup has no Pen frame.** It is built from the workspace's existing form patterns.
8. **Item images:** the product's main image, as on templates, rather than frame 77's per-template photography.
9. **Self-order at 375 px (frame 32):** the frame shows the item grid and the product sheet as two screens, with a large hero photo and a "Hi Adaeze — pick your 2027 kit" greeting. Here the grid and the sheet share one page (side by side from 1024 px): choosing a card fills the sheet. There is no hero photo or greeting.
10. **"APPROVED FOR YOU · 3":** this is a section heading ("Approved for you · 1") rather than the small accent eyebrow, so the page keeps its heading order.
11. **Frame 77's "Ordering for: Lekki" header chip:** the location shows in the heading ("Approved products for Lekki") and the rail ("Delivery to Lekki"). A buyer with more than one location gets an "Ordering for" select.
12. **Long organization names:** the seed's "ACME Hospitality Group (TEST DATA)" makes "Set by …" and "Private · for … people" wrap. Frame 32 uses "ACME".

## Questions / conflicts found

1. **Who pays for an individual self-order pick without an allowance?** J09 says "Allowance, approval and payment apply as configured". Without an allowance (T12-06b), an INDIVIDUAL pick follows the template-order route, so a direct price goes to the employee's cart and checkout. The organization-pays decision (2026-10-10) was about group collections. **Owner to confirm** whether individual store picks should always become an order request or quote for the organization (never the employee's own checkout). If so, T12-06b's allowance can decide when the organization pays directly.
2. **Who may self-order:** TECH-03 has no "order from the company store" permission, so picks need "Create orders, projects, order requests". Viewers can see a store but can't pick from it. **Owner to confirm**, or add a store permission in TECH-03.
