# T04-01 — Cart, checkout and delivery rates

Status: DONE (pending CI and merge).

## What was built

### Data (migration `0022_cart_checkout_delivery`, new tables and one column only)
- **`delivery_zones`** (OPS-FR-100–101): name, kind (`AREA`, `REST_OF_NIGERIA`, `INTERNATIONAL`), `covers` (`{ state, lga }` pairs; `lga: null` covers the whole state), pricing (`FIXED` with `rate_minor` in kobo before VAT, or `QUOTE`), transit days and position.
  - A check constraint ties the rate to the pricing.
  - A partial unique index allows exactly one rest-of-Nigeria row and one International row.
- **`carts`** (one per account, or per browser device key, stored hashed like drafts).
- **`cart_items`**: the configuration, its group (`READY` or `QUOTE`), and a snapshot of the design and its price when added (COM-FR-080). There is one row per configuration.
- **`checkouts`**: the ready items as the server repriced them when the checkout started (frozen), contact, address, the zone and its rate as chosen, billing, the kobo totals, and the VAT setting copied at creation.
  - A partial unique index allows **one OPEN checkout per cart**.
- **`configurations.saved_for_later_at`** marks designs moved out of the cart with "Save for later".

No enum values are added to existing types, so the "existing databases catch up in one deploy" migration test keeps passing.

### Rules (`apps/api/src/sales/delivery.ts`, pure and tested)
- **`zoneFor`:** every address resolves to exactly one zone.
  - An LGA match beats a whole-state match.
  - Anything not covered goes to the rest-of-Nigeria zone.
  - Addresses abroad go to International.
  - With no zones set up at all, delivery is priced by staff (Pen 108 empty state).
- **`zoneOverlaps`** refuses overlapping covers:
  - an LGA already in another zone, or a whole state that another zone covers in part or whole;
  - the same cover listed twice in one zone.
  - The message is Pen's: "Eti-Osa is already in Lagos Island. Remove it from one zone."
- **`checkoutTotals`:**
  - Each item's VAT is what the price calculator worked out for it, so VAT-inclusive prices stay as shown.
  - Delivery rates are before VAT, and get VAT when `tax_settings.vat_on_delivery` is on.
  - Delivery is left out of the total until it's known.

### API
- **Cart** (`/v1/cart…`): owner or browser only; anyone else gets 404.
  - `GET` returns the ready group, the quote group, the ready totals, the quote estimate (never in the total) and the saved-for-later list.
  - `POST items` adds a design (COM-FR-080). A design needing a quote goes to the quote group (COM-FR-081).
  - `DELETE items/:id` removes an item; the design stays a draft.
  - `POST items/:id/save-for-later` moves the item back to the customer's saved designs (your decision).
  - `POST quote-request` ("Submit quote items") makes one quote request per quote item, reusing T03-08's Request Final Quote.
  - Signing in moves the browser's cart into the account's.
- **Edit returns to configuration:** a design in the cart stays editable in the configurator (`IN_CART` drafts can be saved). The cart refreshes its copy when the design changes.
- **Checkout** (`/v1/checkouts…`):
  - `POST` needs an Idempotency-Key. It reprices every ready item on the server and freezes the result (TECH-05 §1).
    - With the same items it returns the cart's open checkout, so a double submit makes one.
    - Two simultaneous submits both get the same checkout (unique index plus retry).
    - Changing a design in the cart (quantity, choices), removing it, or sending it for a quote closes any open checkout holding it. A checkout can never be paid for a copy the customer no longer has.
    - With different items, or after 24 hours, the old checkout is abandoned and a new one keeps the customer's earlier answers.
    - An item whose price now needs review moves to the quote group instead of being charged.
  - `PUT :id/{contact,address,delivery,billing}` saves each step with a version check. The server recalculates the total each time and never reads an amount from the browser.
    - A new address resets the delivery choice.
    - The response lists blockers:
      - steps not finished;
      - delivery needs a quote;
      - payments aren't available yet (T04-03).
- **Delivery zones admin** (`/v1/admin/delivery-zones`, PRICING_MANAGE): list, create, change and remove.
  - Writes are serialised with an advisory lock, so two staff can't add overlapping zones at once.
  - Each write is audited (`delivery.zone_created`, `zone_changed`, `zone_removed`).
  - The two fixed rows can change pricing but can't be removed.
- **OpenAPI** documents every route, and `openapi.json` is regenerated.

## Differences from the design

1. **Checkout steps:** Pen 43/44 show five steps: Contact, Delivery address, Shipping, Payment, Review and pay. COM-FR-083 lists Contact, Delivery address, Delivery option, **Billing and VAT**, Payment, Review.
   - The PRD wins, so step 4 is **Billing and VAT**, and payment happens from "Review and pay" once T04-03 adds Paystack. The count stays at five.
2. **"Save my details for next time"** (Pen 43) is left out until accounts exist. It lands with the T09-01 sign-up part planned for this session, not as a disabled box.
3. **Quantity menu:** Pen shows "500 cards ▾". The menu lists the product's standard quantities, but as plain numbers, because products have no unit name ("cards", "pieces") yet.
   - Garment items keep their quantity in the configurator, because it comes from the size grid.
4. **Cart item thumbnails** are plain tiles. Cart items don't carry product images yet; this is the same approach as the order summary thumbnails in Pen 43.
5. **Delivery rate label:** Pen 108 says "Rate (NGN, incl. VAT setting)". The rate is stored before VAT and gets VAT from the VAT setting at checkout, so the label reads "Rate (NGN, before VAT)" with a hint.
6. **Effective from** is read-only "Now". Zones change for new checkouts at once, and existing checkouts keep their copy, as Pen 108's success message says. Scheduled future rates aren't built.
7. **Cart "Pay today" rail** shows "Delivery: Set at checkout" rather than Pen's "Delivery to Lagos (estimate) ₦4,500". There is no address before checkout, and guessing a zone would show a wrong total for most people.
8. **Checkout payment states** (Pen 45 B–D: processing, failed and success) belong to T04-03 and T04-05.

## Questions / conflicts found

- **Delivery-quote addresses:** when an address falls in a "Quote required" zone, checkout completes every step but payment stays blocked with "We'll send the delivery price before you pay".
  - How the delivery quote reaches the customer (a quote for delivery only, or a staff price on the checkout) isn't specified. Suggest deciding in T04-03/T05-03.
- **LGA lists:** only Lagos (20 LGAs) and FCT (6 area councils) are split by LGA, and other states are covered whole. Add a state's LGAs to `NIGERIA_LGAS` before splitting it into zones.
- **Real rates:** the zone rates and transit days are TEST DATA from Pen 108. They're on the attention list.
- **Several quote items** each become their own quote request (and email). A combined request would fit better with Projects (T05-01).

## Suggestions (out of scope)
- Product unit names ("cards", "pieces") for the cart quantity menu and receipts.
- Product images on cart and checkout items.
- Scheduled future delivery rates, if operations needs them.
