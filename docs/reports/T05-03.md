## What was built

### Submitting a project (Pen 124)
- **The Review step** ("Check your project before we quote") ends with **Request Project Quote**, the only place to submit (PROJ-FR-010).
  - Guests give the email the quote goes to ("No account needed"); signed-in customers use their account's.
  - States:
    - "Sending…";
    - "One thing to fix" (no email);
    - "Not sent. Nothing is lost — try again";
    - a refusal when the project has nothing to quote.
- **Submit** (`POST /v1/projects/:id/submit`, idempotent) runs in one transaction under the project's row lock:
  - the project gets **HP-P-YYYY-NNNN**;
  - it becomes SUBMITTED with a frozen copy of the brief (TECH-01 §5);
  - a **quote starts in PENDING_REVIEW** (HP-Q…, one per project);
  - the customer gets a **"We've got your project"** email with the project's secure link.
- **Pressing more than once** returns the same project and reference.
- **The project's link** shows the confirmation (Pen 124): reference, quote status, and what happens next.
  - The builder pages of a submitted project lead there too, so it can't change.

### The staff queue (Pen 124B, `/projects`)
- **New requests, oldest first:**
  - submitted projects (SUBMITTED, UNDER_REVIEW);
  - **configuration quote requests** (PROJ-FR-012, PENDING_REVIEW or REVISION_REQUESTED);
  - a **Quoted** tab for both.
- **The detail panel** shows **everything the customer sent:**
  - every answer by question;
  - items with quantities and reasons;
  - files and which item they're for;
  - open items;
  - contact, and whether it came from a guest.
- **Start review** moves SUBMITTED → UNDER_REVIEW (audited). **Build quote** opens the quote.

### The quote builder (Pen 111, `/quotes/[id]`)
- **Versions** V1, V2… with tabs. Sent versions are read-only.
- **New version:** copies the latest version into a DRAFT; the first one starts from the admin defaults.
- **Lines:**
  - added from the project's chosen items (suggestions and the customer's own) or from the configuration, with any catalogue price filled in;
  - or lines of your own.
  - Each line has an item, configuration summary, quantity and unit price.
- **Fees:** design, delivery, installation and other, with notes.
- **Currency:** NGN, or USD only when switched on in Payment settings.
- **Also on each version:** valid-for days, terms, assumptions and the production sequence.
- **Totals are always worked out by the server:**
  - VAT applies to the items and fees;
  - delivery is taxed only when the tax setting says so, as at checkout.
- **Payment schedule editor:**
  - deposit, balance or milestones, as percentages or fixed amounts;
  - due on acceptance, before production, before dispatch or on a date;
  - it must add up to the total exactly, with rounding going to the last percentage.
- **Send VN** asks for confirmation ("V1 becomes SUPERSEDED"). Then, in one transaction:
  - the previous sent version becomes SUPERSEDED;
  - this one becomes SENT with valid-until and a frozen copy;
  - the quote becomes READY and the project QUOTED;
  - the **`quote.expire`** job (at valid-until) and the **expiring reminder** (`reminder.send`, a set number of days before) are queued;
  - the customer gets **"Your quote is ready"** (or "revised quote") with the quote's secure link;
  - the send is audited.
- **A sent version never changes:**
  - the service refuses it (409 VERSION_SENT);
  - a **database trigger** refuses any change to a sent version or its lines, or deleting one;
  - only the status may move on (SUPERSEDED, ACCEPTED, DECLINED, EXPIRED).
- **One SENT and one DRAFT per quote at most**, enforced by partial unique indexes.
- **The quotes list** (`/quotes`) has status tabs.

### Quote settings (`/quotes/settings`)
- Validity (14 days to start), the reminder (3 days before), the **default payment schedule** (50% on acceptance, 50% before dispatch to start) and default terms.
- Saving needs QUOTE_MANAGE and a **recent authenticator check** (the step-up dialog). It's version-checked and audited.
- This fills the "default schedule" gap noted in T04-05.

### Jobs (worker)
- **`quote.expire`:** a version still SENT at its valid-until becomes EXPIRED, and so does a READY quote. Safe to repeat.
- **`reminder.send` (QUOTE_EXPIRING):** emails "Your quote expires soon" only if the version is still the sent one and the quote still READY.

### Data (migration `0029_quote_versions`)
- **New tables:** `quote_versions`, `quote_line_items`, `quote_settings` (one row).
- **Changed tables:** `quotes.project_id` (unique); `projects.submitted_snapshot` and `contact`.
- **Also:** the `project_reference_seq` sequence, the default settings row, and the two triggers.
- **Emails:** `project_submitted`, `quote_ready` and `quote_expiring`. All carry secure links, so they can't be resent from the notification log.

### Permissions
- **Customers:** submitting needs the project's owner, browser or link; everyone else gets 404.
- **Staff:** the queue, project detail, quotes and settings need **QUOTE_MANAGE**; staff without it get 403 and customers 404.
- **Navigation:** the Projects section now shows only for QUOTE_MANAGE; it showed for CUSTOMER_SUPPORT too, but its screens need QUOTE_MANAGE.

## Questions and conflicts
1. **Quote link page:** the quote's secure link lands on `/quotes/:id`, the customer quote page that T05-05 builds next. Until then the link answers 404 on dev. It is not deployed to customers before T05-05 merges.
2. **VAT on quotes:** charged on the items, design, installation and other fees, and on delivery only when the tax setting says so. This matches Pen 111's figures and checkout. Unit prices on quotes are before VAT.
3. **Lists:** the queue and the quotes list show at most 100 rows, with no paging yet. Suggestion: cursor paging when volumes need it (T13-03 operations).
4. **Configuration quotes:** a configuration quote's lines come from its saved copy; the unit price is the goods total ÷ quantity, before VAT.
