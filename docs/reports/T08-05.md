## What it does
- **Who gets a courier price.** A Nigerian address with no fixed zone rate ("Other Nigerian states", or no zones at all) gets a live courier price when the address is saved at checkout. The customer can pay straight away.
- **Which courier.**
  - Only couriers that collect from the workshop count; drop-off services are left out.
  - Staff choose the cheapest or the fastest.
  - The price is Shipbubble's total minus its VAT, so the VAT-on-delivery setting applies as it does for zone rates, and VAT isn't counted twice.
- **The parcel.**
  - Each line's weight is its quantity × the unit weight of the product, or of its chosen size.
  - Boxes hold "units per box" units each. They're stacked on the largest footprint, and that is sent as the package size.
  - The pickup date is the next working day; Shipbubble books at most 7 days ahead.
- **When it falls back to staff pricing (#80).** Any of the following sends the checkout to staff pricing as before:
  - courier prices are off, or no key is set;
  - the address is abroad;
  - a product has no weight or box;
  - Shipbubble errors or takes longer than 8 seconds;
  - no courier collects for that address.

  Staff see the reason in Delivery prices.
- **What is kept.** The courier's offer (name, service, delivery estimate and Shipbubble's rate reference) stays on the checkout for booking later (T08-06). The rate reference never reaches the browser.

## Built
- **Migration `0038_courier_prices`:**
  - `products.shipping_weight_grams`, `package_cm` and `units_per_package`;
  - the same weight and box override on `product_option_values`, for sizes;
  - the `courier_settings` table.
- **API:**
  - `sales/courier.ts`: the Shipbubble adapter (address check, rates, categories), the fake courier, the parcel calculation and the courier choice.
  - `sales/courier-pricing.ts`: the settings, and the price used by `setAddress`.
  - `GET`/`PUT /v1/admin/courier-settings` and `GET …/categories` (PRICING_MANAGE, audited). The pickup address is checked with Shipbubble when saved, and its address code is kept.
- **Config:** `SHIPBUBBLE_API_KEY`. It must be `sb_sandbox_` outside production and `sb_prod_` in production. If it's unset, couriers aren't used. See `.env.example`.
- **Admin:**
  - Delivery & installation → Courier prices (on or off, courier choice, package type, pickup contact and address);
  - product editor → Shipping weight and box;
  - Delivery prices shows why no courier price was found.
- **Web:** checkout's delivery step shows "Courier delivery · {courier}", the price before VAT and the courier's estimate after production.
- **Seed:** weights and boxes for every product that ships (below).

## Tests (`sales/courier.test.ts`, written first, fake courier)
- **The parcel:** weights add up; boxes stack on the largest footprint.
- **A Kano address:**
  - gets the cheapest collecting courier, ₦4,500 before VAT (₦4,837.50 with VAT), and the drop-off service is skipped;
  - the payment can start;
  - the rate reference is stored but never sent to the browser.
- **The fastest rule** picks the fastest courier.
- **Fallbacks to staff pricing:**
  - a courier error, with the reason kept for staff;
  - a missing weight, without calling the courier;
  - abroad, and courier prices switched off, without calling the courier.
- **Zone addresses** keep the zone rate and never call the courier.
- **Courier settings:**
  - the pickup address is checked and its code kept;
  - an address the courier can't find is refused on the field;
  - 404 for customers and 403 for staff without PRICING_MANAGE.

## Questions and notes
1. **The price is an estimate made at checkout.** Production takes days, and Shipbubble's rate reference lasts 7 days. When the courier is booked (T08-06), the rate is checked again; if it went up, HolyPearls covers the difference or staff contact the customer. **Owner to decide** which.
2. **Shipbubble's "best value" pick isn't in its API's documented fields,** so the rule offers cheapest or fastest.
3. **Box stacking is an approximation.** Couriers re-weigh at pickup, and Shipbubble charges any difference from the wallet.
4. **Production doesn't refuse to start without a key** (the plan said it would). Without a key, courier prices are simply off and staff price those addresses, so a missing key can't stop orders.
5. **Owner to do:**
   - add the sandbox key to `.env` and to staging Render;
   - in Admin → Delivery & installation → Courier prices, enter the workshop's pickup address, choose a package type, and switch courier prices on.
