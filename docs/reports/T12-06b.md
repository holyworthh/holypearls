## What was built

- **Branch:** `feature/t12-06b-allowances`. **PR:** https://github.com/holyworthh/holypearls/pull/109.
- **Migration `0052_allowances.sql`:**
  - **`allowance_policies`:** one per store. Kind (NONE, PER_ORDER, PER_PERIOD), name, amount, currency, period (MONTH, QUARTER, YEAR) and renewal day (`MM-DD`, Lagos time). Checks require an amount for limits, and a period and day for per-period allowances.
  - **`allowance_ledger`:** what each member's picks committed. Each entry holds the store, membership, period start, amount, design and the order request (if any). Each design appears at most once.
- **Contracts (`groups.ts`):**
  - The allowance as the person sees it: amount, used, left and when it renews.
  - The update input.
  - Store items gain `covered` and `pickTotalMinor`.
  - Store setup shows the saved allowance.
- **API (`groups/stores.ts`):**
  - **`PUT /stores/:id/allowance` (MANAGE_STORES):** sets no limit, a per-order limit, or an amount per period from a renewal day. It is versioned with the store and audited (`store.allowance_updated`, with before and after).
  - **Periods (`allowancePeriod`):**
    - The current period starts on the most recent renewal day in Lagos time. Yearly periods step 12 months, quarterly 3 and monthly 1, from the renewal month.
    - The ledger keeps each entry's period start. A new period therefore starts at the full amount, and old entries stay in their own period.
  - **Balance:** a member's commitments this period.
    - An entry whose order request was rejected or cancelled is given back. `organizations/approvals.ts` exports `releasedRequests`.
    - What's left never goes below zero.
  - **Picks in self-order stores with individual routing:**
    - **Within the allowance:** the organization pays. The pick goes to HolyPearls as a quote request for the organization, never to the employee's own checkout. The organization's own approval rule still applies.
    - **Over the allowance, or with no price yet:** the pick becomes an order request for approval, whatever the organization's rule (Pen 78 C). The note reads, for example, "This order is ₦4,500 over Adaeze's uniform allowance (₦32,500 left)."
    - **Per-period stores:** every pick is recorded in the ledger at its estimated total, with its order request. The check and the entry happen together under a lock on the store's allowance, so two picks at once can't both spend the same balance.
    - **Per-order limits:** compare one order's total, and keep no balance.
  - **The shared template-order route (`placeTemplateLines`)** takes an `allowance` option: the over-limit note forces approval, and an allowance stops checkout. It now also returns the order request id.
  - **OpenAPI:** the new route is documented.
- **Customer site:**
  - **Self-order store (Pen 32, 78 B):**
    - The allowance card: "₦150,000 of ₦150,000 left", a bar, and "Uniform allowance · renews 1 Jan" (or "Up to ₦… per order").
    - "Covered" on items that fit.
    - The button reads "Place order", with "Paid from your allowance. ₦… will be left."
  - **Over the allowance (Pen 78 C):**
    - "This order is ₦… over your allowance".
    - "Allowance left ₦… · this order ₦…" (or "Limit per order").
    - "Paying the difference yourself isn't enabled by ACME."
    - "Ask my manager to approve", which opens the order request.
  - **Store setup:** an Allowance section for self-order stores with individual routing. It has the limit kind, name, amount in naira, how often it renews, and the month and day it renews from.

## Differences from the design or PRD, and why

1. **One item per pick:** frame 78 B shows a basket ("Polo + Apron ₦15,000"). Picks are one item each (T12-06), so each pick is checked against what's left. A basket is a suggested follow-up.
2. **"Remove an item" (frame 78 C):** with one item per pick there's nothing to remove; the employee can choose another item instead. "Pay the difference" isn't offered: the ticket doesn't build it and the frame shows it disabled ("isn't enabled by ACME").
3. **Collection-routed stores show no allowance.** Picks join a group collection with no price per person until it's quoted. The organization approves the Order Draft as a whole (T12-03b).
4. **Procurement stores have no allowance.** The organization's approval rule already covers buyers (frame 77).
5. **"Tell me when it's back" (frame 78 D)** is still not built (see T12-06).
6. **Picks with no price** (quote-only products) need approval when an allowance is set, as the PRD says ("going over the limit asks for approval"; an unknown amount can't be shown to be under it). They are recorded in the ledger at ₦0 until priced.
7. **The commitment is the estimate at the time of the pick.** It isn't adjusted if HolyPearls' quote differs.

## Questions / conflicts found

1. **T12-06 question 1 (who pays for individual picks)** is partly answered here: with an allowance, the organization pays (a quote for the organization, never the employee's checkout). Without one, a direct price still goes to the employee's checkout. **Owner to confirm** whether stores with no allowance should also always bill the organization.
2. **Changing the renewal day or period mid-period** starts counting from the new period start, so earlier entries no longer count. **Owner decision (2026-10-19):** counting starts from the new period. This is built as described.
3. **Two picks at the same moment:** fixed at the owner's request (2026-10-19). The balance check and the ledger entry happen together while the store's allowance row is locked. The pick is placed after that, and if placing fails the reservation is removed. The lock is per store, so picks in one store wait for each other briefly. A per-member lock would avoid this if stores get busy.
