## What was built

- **Branch:** `feature/t12-03b-order-draft`. **PR:** see the tracker row.
- **Migration `0049_order_drafts.sql`, `order_drafts`:** the collection, status (CURRENT, STALE, SUBMITTED), and a saved copy of the totals and every included participant's answers. Once submitted, it also holds the design it became and its order request or quote, plus who made it, when it was submitted, and `version`.
- **Contracts (`groups.ts`):** the Order Draft, the draft state (current plus the number of stale drafts) and the staff origin view. `adminQuoteSchema` gains `orderDraftId`.
- **API:**
  - **`groups/order-drafts.ts`:**
    - **Make** (READY_TO_ORDER → ORDER_DRAFT_CREATED): totals by size (counting quantities), with names, and by delivery location, over the people in the order who sent their answers. The excluded and removed rows are listed with why.
    - **Submit** (`configurationForGroup` in the configuration module):
      - Builds one design from the collection's source: the size split, a personalisation row per named piece, and the template's controls with nothing editable.
      - With the organization's approval rule (T11-04) applying, it becomes an order request held for approval. Otherwise it's a quote request placed for the collection's location, owned by the organizer.
      - The draft becomes SUBMITTED. Nothing is ordered until a quote is accepted.
    - **Staff origin** (`GET /v1/admin/order-drafts/:id`, QUOTE_MANAGE): pieces from participant links, rows per import (T12-03c fills these), and the excluded rows with reasons. Read only.
  - **Reopen (B2B-FR-055):**
    - With a draft not yet sent, reopening marks it STALE and the collection opens. After closing again, a new draft is made.
    - With a sent draft, reopening is refused (`DRAFT_SUBMITTED`: "Changes go through a change request and may need a new quote and re-approval").
    - Removing people is refused while a draft exists (reopen first).
  - **The staff quote view** gains `orderDraftId`.
  - **OpenAPI:** the 4 routes are documented.
- **Customer site:**
  - **The collection page** shows the Order Draft panel above the dashboard:
    - "Make the Order Draft" when ready.
    - Totals by size, with names and by location, and the excluded count.
    - The "Next step" note and "Submit the Order Draft".
    - After sending: "With HolyPearls for a quote (HP-Q-…)", or "Waiting for your organization's approval" with a link to the request.
    - A STALE note after reopening.
  - **The reopen dialog** warns that the Order Draft will be marked STALE.
- **Staff admin:**
  - **"Where these N pieces came from"** (`/order-drafts/[id]`, frame 133 u3BRd): sources, excluded rows, and the read-only note.
  - **The quote builder** links to it ("Where this group order came from") when a quote came from a group.

## Permission rules added

- **Making and submitting drafts:** `MANAGE_COLLECTIONS` for the collection's location. A Viewer gets 403.
- **Seeing the draft:** whoever can see the collection.
- **The staff origin view:** `QUOTE_MANAGE` (the people who quote the group order). Others get 403, and customers 404.

## Differences from the design or PRD, and why

1. **Draft number "OD-0031" (frame 133 staff view):** drafts don't have a number yet. The staff page is reached from the quote, which has its reference.
2. **"Organization approval given" and "HolyPearls quote issued" reopen cases (B2B-FR-055):** both arise only after submitting, so reopening a sent draft is refused with the change-request message. The change-request flow itself isn't built yet.
3. **"Production started" read-only (B2B-FR-055):** after the quote is accepted the order follows the normal order flow, and participant data can no longer be changed here.
