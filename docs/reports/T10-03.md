### Data (migration 0041)

- **Columns:** `checkouts`, `orders`, `projects` and `quotes` gain `organization_id` and `location_id`.
  - Both nullable, and both set or both null (a check constraint). Null means a personal purchase.
  - A composite foreign key (`location_id`, `organization_id`) → `locations`, so a record can't name another organization's location.
  - Indexes (`organization_id`, `location_id`).
- **Who placed it:** the existing `owner_user_id` already records the person who placed the record.
- **Inheritance (TECH-03 §7, "child records inherit"):**
  - A checkout's organization and location go to its order.
  - A project's go to its quote, and the quote's go to the order made when it is accepted.
  - Proofs, receipts and shipments follow their order.

### Placing for an organization ("placing orders and projects for the organization and location")

- **Checkout:** `PUT /v1/checkouts/:id/placement` with `{ organizationId, locationId }` or `null`.
  - The person needs an ACTIVE membership with `CREATE_REQUESTS`, and the location must be inside their scope.
  - Only on an OPEN checkout, with the version check.
  - The checkout page shows "Who's this order for?" only to people who can place for an organization.
- **Project:** `PUT /v1/projects/:id/placement`, the same rules, while the project is a DRAFT. The project builder's review step shows the same choice.
- **Quotes requested from the configurator or the cart** stay personal at launch (recorded as a follow-up).

### Workspace reads (organizations module, scoped)

- **Scope:** the member's locations (or one chosen location from the switcher), and `VIEW_RECORDS`. Without `VIEW_RECORDS` (Buyers), only records they placed. Outside the member's scope is 404.
- **Routes:**
  - `GET /v1/organizations/:id/overview?location=`:
    - Needs Attention: proofs awaiting approval, quotes ready, balances due, payment problems.
    - Active work: open orders and projects.
    - Counts for the navigation.
  - `GET …/orders?view=attention|active|complete|all&location=`
  - `GET …/projects?location=`, grouped DRAFT / QUOTE PENDING / QUOTE READY / ACTIVE ORDER / COMPLETE (frame 64).
  - `GET …/billing?location=` (`VIEW_BILLING`, Owner and Admin by default; others 403): balances due, receipts and refunds across the organization's orders.
  - `GET …/search?q=&location=`: orders and projects by number, reference or item. Templates, assets and groups join in T11 and T12.
- **Opening the records:** an organization order or project opens for a member who can see it (`VIEW_RECORDS` and location, or the person who placed it), read only. Approving proofs and accepting quotes stay with the person who placed it until T11-04 (quote authority).

## Permission rules added

- **Workspace views:** every view uses the member's scope. A location outside it (or another organization's) is 404, never an empty list. Buyers (no `VIEW_RECORDS`) see only what they placed.
- **Billing:** `VIEW_BILLING`, held by Owner and Admin. Others get 403 (B2B-FR-012).
- **Search:** searches only inside the scope (B2B-FR-013).
- **Placing a checkout or project:** an ACTIVE member with `CREATE_REQUESTS`, at a location they hold. Someone else's checkout is 404.
- **Reading organization orders:** read only. A deactivated member loses it on their next request.

## Differences from the design or PRD, and why

1. **Templates, Groups, Brand Assets and Company Store** (sidebar items, the overview's Templates and Groups panels, the "Order from Template" and "Create Group Collection" quick actions, and search groups for templates, assets and groups) arrive with T11 and T12. The quick actions are "Start a Project" and "Order products".
2. **Active work:** the Pen's single "Active orders & projects" panel is two panels, Active orders and Active projects, so each has its own "View all". There are no product thumbnails, because order cards don't carry an image yet.
3. **Orders table at 1440:** frame 63 has a five-column table. The rows here keep the same fields in one responsive row (number · location · date, title, next step, chips, Open), which also gives the Pen's 375 px cards.
4. **"Order from a template" next step** and the reorder action on delivered orders aren't shown in lists. Reorder stays on the order page.
5. **Payment history** shows receipts. Refunds and participant payments arrive with T13-05 and T12.
6. **Another member's projects** are listed but open only for the person who placed them ("Placed by a colleague"), and organization orders open read only. Acting on someone else's record (approving proofs, paying, accepting quotes) is quote authority, T11-04.
7. **Quotes requested from the configurator or the cart** stay personal; only checkouts and projects can be placed for an organization.
8. **Workspace lists build each order card with a few small queries.** Lists stop at 50 (overview 30, billing 200). Worth a look in the P14 query-plan review.
9. **Billing location:** billing is at `/workspace/[id]/billing`, a tab under Team & Settings, as frame 130 labels it.
