## What was built

### Data (migration `0027_orders_receipts`)
- **`orders`**:
  - Numbered `HP-2026-0001` from `order_number_seq`, with **one order per checkout** (`checkout_id` unique).
  - Copies the totals, contact, address, delivery and billing as paid, and who may open it (account or browser, from the checkout).
- **`order_items`**:
  - The checkout's frozen items, including the `OrderableConfigurationSnapshotV1` unchanged (review P1-06).
  - Status `AWAITING_PREREQUISITE` (TECH-01 §4 enum; production moves it from T07-01).
- **`receipts`**:
  - Numbered `HP-R-2026-0001`, **one per successful attempt**.
  - Records the amount, currency, Paystack reference, method, payer, the lines as receipted, and when it was paid.
- **`payment_settings`** gains `account_supports` (what staff confirm the Paystack account supports, with who and when) and `checklist` (TECH-05 §14).

### When a payment succeeds (`payments.ts` → `orders/orders.ts`)
- In the same transaction that credits the payment:
  - the checkout completes;
  - the order is made from its snapshot;
  - the receipt is made from the attempt;
  - an **"Order confirmed"** email is queued with the receipt number, the lines, and the order's **secure link** (guest link, purpose ORDER, 180 days).
- **Safe to repeat:**
  - A replayed webhook, a late check, or several results at once all return the same order.
  - The unique `checkout_id` and `attempt_id` are the last safeguards.
- **When the order is made:** a staff decision to keep a payment that wasn't credited yet (an amount mismatch or an unclear result) also makes the order, receipt and email.
- **The worker** sends the email with the customer site's address (`BETTER_AUTH_URL`, now read by the worker).

### Customer pages (web)
- **Payment received → "Order confirmed"** (Pen 45 D) with the order number, "We've emailed your receipt" and **View your order and receipt**.
- **`/orders/[id]`** (Pen 52, confirmation part only):
  - number and paid date, items with their status chip, and the stage tracker (Paid → Proof now → Production → Quality Check → Dispatch with the estimate);
  - delivery address, the receipt link, and Get help.
  - The account, the browser that paid, or the order's link can open it; anyone else gets 404.
- **`/orders/[id]/receipts/[receiptId]`:** the receipt from its record, with **Download PDF**.
  - The PDF (`GET /v1/orders/:id/receipts/:receiptId/pdf`) is laid out by MuPDF, already in the API, so there's no new dependency.

### Admin (apps/admin, ADR-017)
- **Payments list** (Pen 109): every attempt with reference, what it was for, customer, amount, attempt status and what the customer sees. Search by reference, order or customer, a status filter, paging, and a paused notice.
- **Intent detail** (Pen 97): attempts, Paystack events, every check, a summary (with the receipt) and the audit history. It links to the order and to an open review.
- **Review queue** (Pen 99): open reviews oldest first, and resolved ones. The detail shows what happened.
  - The decision is either **keep a successful payment** or **return to OPEN**, with a **note required**. It's audited, and Paystack records are never deleted.
  - Returning to OPEN is refused once an order exists ("refund the extra payment instead").
  - Staff with PAYMENT_VIEW only see "View only".
- **Payment settings** (Pen 100):
  - online payments on or paused, the channels passed to Paystack, international cards, USD quotes;
  - what the account supports (confirmed by staff, with name and date), the setup checklist, and recent changes.
  - **Saving needs a recent authenticator check:** the new **step-up dialog** opens on `RECENT_MFA_REQUIRED` and retries after a correct code.
  - Methods the account doesn't support can't be switched on (API rule and UI).
  - Pausing shows what customers see and how many attempts are still being checked.

### Permissions
- **PAYMENT_VIEW:** list, detail, reviews and settings (read).
- **PAYMENT_MANAGE:** decisions and settings changes, which are also audited with the permission.
- **Test people:** Ada gains PAYMENT_MANAGE; the new **Folake** (TEST DATA) has PAYMENT_VIEW only.

## Tests

- **`apps/api/src/orders/orders.test.ts`:**
  - **Orders:** the order is made from the snapshot; four parallel results make **one order, one receipt and one email**; strangers, other customers and unknown ids get 404.
  - **Email and link:** the confirmation email's link redeems to a guest session that opens **only that order**.
  - **Receipt:** the receipt page and PDF (`%PDF-`, attachment name) come from the record.
  - **Payments list and detail:** for PAYMENT_VIEW; 403 without payment permissions; 404 for customers.
  - **Reviews:** a note is required; view-only staff get 403; keeping a payment makes the order once and is audited with PAYMENT_MANAGE; a second decision gets 409; returning to OPEN leaves the Paystack record and makes no order.
  - **Settings:** unsupported methods are refused until staff confirm the account; pausing blocks new attempts and appears in recent changes; view-only staff and stale versions are refused.
- **`apps/api/src/payments/payments.test.ts`:** updated for the new signatures; all T04-03 tests still apply.
- **`apps/web/e2e/payments-admin.spec.ts`:**
  - the admin payments list, review queue and settings pass axe with no sideways scroll at 375 and 1440;
  - another person's order page is 404.

## Questions and conflicts

1. **One email, not two:** the receipt email and the order link (PRD COM-FR-085, PAY-FR-006) are one "Order confirmed" email. Because it carries the order's secure link, it can't be resent from the notification log; the receipt page and PDF can be opened again from the link.
2. **Receipt PDF currency:** MuPDF's built-in font has no naira sign, so the PDF shows "NGN 47,300.00". The page and email use ₦. Embedding a font is possible later.
3. **Returning a credited payment to OPEN** is refused once its order exists. Pen 99 offers it for duplicates, but undoing an order belongs with refunds and cancellation (T13-05). I recorded this rather than build it half.
4. **Staging:** online payments are off until someone switches them on in the new settings page. Then the test-mode check from T04-03 can be run (see ATTENTION 24).
