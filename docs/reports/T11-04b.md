## What was built

- **Branch:** `feature/t11-04b-quote-reapproval`. **PR:** see the tracker row. No migration: a quote is linked to its order request through `order_requests.quote_id` (T11-04).
- **API:**
  - **`organizationQuoteCheck` (organizations module), used by the quote page and by accepting:**
    - **Who may accept:** an organization's quote can be accepted only by someone with "Accept HolyPearls quotes" for its location (B2B-FR-044). Otherwise the blocker is "Only people who can accept HolyPearls quotes for ACME … can accept this quote", and accepting is refused with 403 `ACCEPT_QUOTES_REQUIRED`. This is checked before the email code, so an Approver can never accept.
    - **The approved amount:** a quote from an order request is checked against the latest APPROVED amount. Above it, acceptance is blocked with **"Organization re-approval required"** (QUOTE-FR-021): 422 `ORGANIZATION_REAPPROVAL_REQUIRED`. The quote keeps its status (READY) and stays visible and unchanged.
    - **While re-approval is pending:** "Accept unlocks once ACME … approves ₦…". If the organization declines, acceptance stays blocked.
  - **Seeing the quote:** an organization's quote opens for members who can see its location: `VIEW_RECORDS`, or the requester (T10-03's `canSeeOrganizationRecord`). Others still get 404.
  - **`POST …/order-requests/:id/reapproval`** (the requester, or someone who can accept quotes): only when the latest sent version is above the approved amount (`WITHIN_APPROVAL` otherwise).
    - The request goes back to PENDING_APPROVAL with the quote's total as the estimate and an optional note ("what changed in the quote").
    - The approvers are emailed, and it's audited.
  - **Decisions on a re-approval:**
    - Approving inserts a new APPROVED record for the new total; the first one is kept. The request returns to SENT_TO_HOLYPEARLS, with no new quote.
    - Declining marks it REJECTED.
    - "Ask for changes" records CHANGES_REQUESTED and leaves the quote with HolyPearls.
  - **Views:** the customer quote gains `organizationApproval` (approved, approver, current total, difference, pending, can send). The order request gains `reapproval` and `quoteId`. The new blocker codes are in the contracts.
  - **OpenAPI:** the re-approval route is documented.
- **Customer site:**
  - **Quote page (frame 48):** "New approval needed before you can accept", with ACME approved / approved by, Current HolyPearls quote, and Difference ("Above the approved amount"). The rail adds REAPPROVAL REQUIRED, the note, and "Send for reapproval", with Accept disabled and the reason under it.
  - **Approval review (frame 73B):** a REAPPROVAL REQUIRED chip; "HolyPearls' quote came back higher" with You approved, HolyPearls quote and Difference; "What changed in the quote" (the requester's note); and "View HolyPearls quote". Approve ₦… shows the new total.
- **T10-03's placement test now has the Owner accept the Lekki project's quote.** The Location Manager is refused, as B2B-FR-044 and TECH-03 §7 require; T10-03's report said acting on organization records waited for T11-04.

## Permission rules added

- **Accepting an organization's quote:** `ACCEPT_QUOTES` for its location. The Approver (`APPROVE_REQUESTS` only), Location Managers, Buyers and Viewers get 403 `ACCEPT_QUOTES_REQUIRED`.
- **Seeing an organization's quote:** members with `VIEW_RECORDS` for its location, or its requester. A Victoria Island Buyer gets 404 on a Lekki quote.
- **Sending for re-approval:** the requester, or someone with `ACCEPT_QUOTES`.
- **Deciding a re-approval:** as in T11-04 (`APPROVE_REQUESTS` for the location, no self-approval with separation on).

## Differences from the design or PRD, and why

1. **"Send this quote to Bola Martins" (frame 48):** names the organization's approvers as a group ("Send this quote to ACME's approvers"). Every approver for the location is emailed, as in T11-04.
2. **"Request changes instead" in frame 48's rail:** the quote page's existing "Request changes" already covers asking HolyPearls for changes.
3. **The ₦450,000 seed fixture (TECH-08 §11):** the scenario is built in the API test. The seed has no quote fixtures yet (quotes need staff-built versions), so seeding it waits for quote seed data (suggested below).
4. **Scope changes (QUOTE-FR-021 "or the scope has changed in a way the policy flags"):** only the amount is checked. No policy option flags scope changes yet, so there's nothing to compare against.

## Questions / conflicts found

1. **TECH-01 §4, re-approval states:** the request goes SENT_TO_HOLYPEARLS → PENDING_APPROVAL → SENT_TO_HOLYPEARLS for a re-approval, and REJECTED if declined. TECH-01 lists neither transition. **Proposed amendment:** "SENT_TO_HOLYPEARLS → PENDING_APPROVAL (HolyPearls' quote above the approved amount; re-approval); PENDING_APPROVAL → SENT_TO_HOLYPEARLS (re-approved) or REJECTED".
2. **Who accepts an organization quote placed by a Buyer or Location Manager:** by TECH-03 §7, only Owners and Admins (or anyone the Owner grants it to). That changes T10-03's behaviour, where the person who placed a project could accept its quote. **Owner to confirm**, or grant "Accept HolyPearls quotes" to the people who should.
