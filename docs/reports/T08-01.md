## Plan as built
1. **Shipments (OPS-FR-070–073).**
   - Staff create one or more shipments from items that are READY_TO_DISPATCH, with destination, carrier and tracking number.
   - "Ship now" can't exceed what is ready and not already in a shipment ("Only 40 are ready. Ship 40 or fewer.").
   - Items waiting for a balance (READY_AWAITING_RELEASE) can't be shipped (OPS-FR-064).
   - Status is set by hand, along TECH-01 §4 only.
   - Each change is a new `shipment_events` row, so history is never erased.
2. **Item statuses follow units, not shipments.**
   - An item becomes DISPATCHED once every unit is in a dispatched shipment, and DELIVERED once every unit is delivered. A partial shipment leaves it where it is.
   - The order summary (T07-01) then shows PARTIALLY_DISPATCHED, DISPATCHED, PARTIALLY_DELIVERED and DELIVERED across items.
   - The customer is emailed on dispatch (carrier and tracking) and on delivery.
3. **Order page (FUL-FR-031, 060).**
   - Shipment cards show "Shipment N of M", status, items × quantity, destination, carrier and tracking, dispatched or delivered date, and status history. Staff notes are never shown.
   - Once delivered: "Order … again" per catalogue item, "Report a problem", and the reports list.
4. **Reorder (FUL-FR-061–062, OPS-FR-018).**
   - The ordered configuration is frozen once ordered, so it's copied into a new draft linked to the order item (`reorder_of_item_id`).
   - **Exact reorder** is offered only when the product is still sold and every chosen option, the artwork, the placement and the production method are unchanged, and it prices directly today. The copy goes to the cart at today's price. The proof is skipped only when `product_rules.proof_skippable_on_reorder` allows it (requirement recorded as `SKIPPED_ON_REORDER`).
   - **Reorder with changes** opens the copy to edit. Any change to what is made (options, quantity, files, sizes, placements) clears `reorder_exact`, so changing the artwork turns it into a reorder with changes and the proof is needed.
   - Items made from a quote can't be reordered online: they start a new project.
5. **Problem reports (FUL-FR-070).**
   - Fields: item, one of five types, how many are affected (no more than ordered), photos (the reporter's own uploads only), and a description.
   - The reference is HP-RP-YYYY-NNNN from a sequence, and an email is sent.
   - Only someone who can open the order can report on it; anyone else gets 404.
   - Staff triage it (CUSTOMER_SUPPORT): NEW → TRIAGED → CLOSED, with an internal note and replies emailed to the customer.

## Tests (`shipments/shipments.test.ts`)
- **Shipments:**
  - not ready, then over-quantity ("Only 250 are ready…");
  - 100 + 150 in two shipments, then nothing more can ship;
  - a skipped status is refused;
  - after the first shipment is dispatched the item stays READY_TO_DISPATCH; after the second it's DISPATCHED (summary DISPATCHED);
  - after the first is delivered it's still DISPATCHED; after both, DELIVERED (summary DELIVERED, reorderable);
  - the dispatch email is queued;
  - **history keeps every event, including two OUT_FOR_DELIVERY rows after an attempted delivery**;
  - customers never see staff notes.
- **A balance-held item can't ship.**
- **FULFILMENT_MANAGE:** 403 for staff without it, 404 for customers.
- **Problem reports:**
  - more than ordered and a missing type are refused;
  - someone else's photo gets 404;
  - **another customer and a stranger get 404**;
  - the reference format, the email and the order view are checked;
  - triage keeps the note internal; the reply is emailed; close; an illegal re-triage is refused;
  - 403 without CUSTOMER_SUPPORT.
- **Reorder:**
  - an exact check that is available with the proof SKIPPED and today's price, and 404 for a stranger;
  - an exact copy in the cart, linked, which when checked out has **its proof NOT_REQUIRED (SKIPPED_ON_REORDER)**;
  - with changes: an editable draft;
  - **editing an exact copy clears reorder_exact**;
  - a rule that doesn't allow skipping gives proof REQUIRED;
  - a product no longer sold means exact isn't available (and the POST is refused).

## Questions and conflicts
1. **Reference format.** Pen says "HP-RP-2026-0012 · format to be confirmed". Built as shown; owner to confirm (ATTENTION 32).
2. **Problem photos need a guest link or an account.** The upload service refuses anonymous uploads. A customer who only has the paying browser can still report without photos, or open the order from their email to add photos.
3. **A returned shipment** (RETURNED) frees its units to be shipped again. An item that was DISPATCHED doesn't move back, because TECH-01 §4 has no such step.
4. **Exact reorder prices at today's price** (PRD-00 §13). The cart shows the final price; the reorder page shows it as "today's price".
