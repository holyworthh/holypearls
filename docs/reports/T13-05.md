## What was built

- **Branch:** `feature/t13-05-refunds`. **PR:** https://github.com/holyworthh/holypearls/pull/118.
- **Migration `0057_refunds.sql`:** the `refunds` table and `refund_number_seq` (RF-0001…). Each row holds:
  - the attempt, intent and (optionally) the review it settles;
  - the amount, currency and reason;
  - the status (PENDING, PROCESSED or FAILED), Paystack's refund id and status word, and the failure;
  - every check with Paystack, who issued it, and when it was processed.
  - It has a database check that the amount is above zero.
- **Payment gateway (`packages/payments`):**
  - **New calls:** `refund`, `refundStatus` and `listRefunds` (Paystack `POST /refund`, `GET /refund/:id`, `GET /refund?transaction=`).
  - **A clear 4xx refusal** is `RefundRefusedError`. Anything else (a 5xx or a timeout) means the outcome is unknown.
  - **Status words:** processed and failed are final; pending, processing and needs-attention are not.
  - **The fake gateway** holds refunds and can refuse one, lose its answer, settle it, and send refund webhooks.
- **API (`payments/refunds.ts`):**
  - **Issuing** (`POST /v1/admin/refunds`): REFUND_ISSUE, a recent MFA check and an Idempotency-Key.
    - **Under a lock on the attempt:** it must be SUCCEEDED, and the amount must not exceed what's left ("Can't refund more than the ₦… left on this payment."). Pending and processed refunds count against the payment.
    - **Saved as PENDING first,** with the audit entry and the refund.check jobs, then Paystack is asked.
    - **Paystack's answer** sets the id and status. A refusal fails the refund ("Paystack declined: …"), and a failed refund doesn't count.
    - **No answer:** it stays PENDING with no id ("Paystack didn't answer…"). The check looks for it in Paystack's list for the transaction instead of asking again. If it still isn't there at the last check, it was never made and fails, freeing the amount.
  - **Status changes** come only from Paystack's refund API:
    - **After a refund webhook:** the Paystack webhook now also takes `refund.*` events, which carry the transaction as `transaction_reference`. They go through the existing `payment.verify` follow-up.
    - **On the `refund.check` schedule:** 10 min, 1 h, 6 h, 1 day and 3 days.
    - **Final statuses** never change again, and replays only add to the check history.
  - **Processed:** the review the refund came from resolves as REFUNDED, with the refund number and reason as its note. A REVIEW_REQUIRED intent with a credited payment returns to SUCCEEDED once nothing else is open.
  - **Customer status (TECH-01 §4):** processed refunds against what was paid give PARTIALLY_REFUNDED or REFUNDED. This applies on the customer's order page, the workspace orders, the payments list and the refunds list.
  - **Untouched:** the attempt, intent, installment and receipt are never changed. Refunds appear on the intent detail as their own section, and in its history (audit entries `refund.issued`, `refund.processed` and `refund.failed`).
  - **Other routes:** `GET /v1/admin/refunds` (list) and `GET /v1/admin/refunds/payments/:attemptId?review=` (the issue form), both REFUND_ISSUE.
  - **OpenAPI:** documented.
- **Admin:**
  - **`/refunds` (frame 121 `NJoG3`):**

## Differences from the design or PRD, and why

1. **The issue form isn't compared in the browser.** The browser tests run without Paystack, so there's no paid payment to refund there. Its behaviour is covered by the API tests, and its markup by the type check. Suggestion: an e2e seed with a paid order (a follow-up).
2. **"PR-0041" review numbers:** reviews have no number, so the form says "FROM PAYMENT REVIEW · DUPLICATE_PAYMENT".
3. **"Refunds start from a payment review or an order":** a refund starts from a successful attempt (on the intent page) or a review. An order's payments are reached through Payments.
4. **A refunded duplicate shows PARTIALLY_REFUNDED to the customer** after the review resolves. TECH-01 §4 counts every refund against everything paid, and both payments were received. **Owner to confirm** whether a refunded duplicate should instead show PAID.
5. **PAID_PARTICIPANT_REMOVED reviews** can be refunded the same way, but nothing opens them yet: participants don't pay individually in the current build (T12).
6. **Refunds stay PENDING after the last check** (3 days) if Paystack hasn't finished. Paystack can take days, and staff see the status on the list.
