## What was built

- **Branch:** `feature/t14-01-structured-data`. **PR:** see the tracker row.
- **ProductGroup variants (`lib/seo.ts`, Stage 3 §49):**
  - A product whose configurator offers more than one colour, size or fabric is published as one ProductGroup.
  - Its fields: `productGroupID` (the slug); `variesBy` (`https://schema.org/color`, `size`, `material`); and `hasVariant`, one Product per combination with its colour, size and material, plus the colour photo when there is one.
  - **No variant has an Offer of its own:** prices are set by the whole design, not per colour or size. The group's "from" Offer, when real (PUB-SEO-003), is the only price stated. Quoted products still publish no Offer.
  - **The list stops at 100 combinations** (marked `ponytail:`). Beyond that the product stays a plain Product.
- **Category hubs:** CollectionPage with the hub's products as an ItemList, plus BreadcrumbList (Home, parent, category). Stage 3 §48 asks for both, and category hubs published neither before.
- **Sitemap index (Stage 3 §53):**
  - `/sitemap.xml` is now a sitemap index of `/sitemaps/pages.xml`, `/sitemaps/categories.xml` and `/sitemaps/products.xml`, each with its newest change date.
  - Pages without editable content carry no date. robots.txt still points to `/sitemap.xml`.
- **Search Console and Bing verification:**
  - The new optional settings `GOOGLE_SITE_VERIFICATION` and `BING_SITE_VERIFICATION` (HTML-tag method) print the `google-site-verification` and `msvalidate.01` tags on every page.
  - Nothing is printed until a token is set. Both settings are added to `.env.example`.
- **Canonical and structured-data check (`apps/web/scripts/seo-check.ts`):** for each page it checks:
  - The canonical address is the page's own sitemap address.
  - The page isn't noindex.
  - Every ld+json block parses, uses the schema.org context, is a type we publish, and has that type's required fields (Google Search Central):
    - Organization, WebSite;
    - BreadcrumbList (positions, names, absolute URLs except the last);
    - Product and ProductGroup (an Offer needs a price above zero, NGN or USD, and an availability);
    - CollectionPage items, Article (headline, date, author), and Service (name, provider).
  - An unexpected type (for example FAQPage, whose rich results ended in 2026) fails.
- **`apps/web/scripts/check-seo.ts`:** runs the same check against any running site, for example staging.

```bash
npx tsx apps/web/scripts/check-seo.ts https://staging.holypearls.example
```

## Differences from the design or PRD, and why

1. **Variants are listed without their own Offer** (see above). Google's merchant listings prefer per-variant prices. They can come with Merchant Center (Stage 3 §53, "later").
2. **Variant URLs:** variants have no address of their own (one page, PUB-SEO-002), so `hasVariant` items carry no `url`.
