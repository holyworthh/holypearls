## Plan as built
1. **Prerequisites (TECH-01 §4).** An item becomes QUEUED, by the system, once:
   - its proof is APPROVED, or NOT_REQUIRED;
   - every deposit installment (ON_ACCEPTANCE or BEFORE_PRODUCTION) is PAID;
   - its configuration's artwork and logo files are READY (no files means nothing to wait for).
   This check runs in the same transaction as whatever meets the last prerequisite: order creation, proof approval, a payment, or a file marked READY.
2. **Staff moves.** Only the moves TECH-01 §4 lists are offered:
   - QUEUE, which re-checks the prerequisites and says what's missing;
   - START (QUEUED → IN_PRODUCTION);
   - TO_QUALITY_CHECK.
   Anything else is refused and nothing changes (ILLEGAL_MOVE, PREREQUISITE_MISSING, ON_HOLD, VERSION_CONFLICT).
3. **Quality check (OPS-FR-063).** A passed check goes to READY_TO_DISPATCH, or to READY_AWAITING_RELEASE while a BEFORE_DISPATCH balance is unpaid. In that case the balance falls DUE at once and the payment_due email is queued. Rework goes back to IN_PRODUCTION. Every result is kept in `qc_results`.
4. **Balance release (FUL-FR-021, TECH-05 §10).** Paying the balance moves READY_AWAITING_RELEASE items to READY_TO_DISPATCH inside the payment's own transaction. There is no manual release.
5. **Holds (OPS-FR-062, FUL-FR-013).** A hold records:
   - reason category, internal note, customer explanation;
   - who acts, and timing impact in working days.
   It pauses the item without changing its status. The item then shows ON_HOLD, and staff can't move it until the hold is resolved. The customer is emailed when a hold is placed and when it's resolved. Only one hold can be open per item.
6. **Order summary (PRD-00 §8).** `orderSummaryStatus()` works the summary out from the items and their holds; it is never stored.

## Tests
- **`production/summary.test.ts`:** a 20-row summary status table.
- **`production/production.test.ts` (5):**
  - **Waiting for the proof:**
    - a manual queue is refused with "the proof is still being prepared (PREPARING)";
    - approval queues the item in its own transaction, recorded as System.
  - **Moves:**
    - only allowed moves are offered;
    - a skipped step and a stale version are refused;
    - rework then pass, with both QC results and the audit entries kept.
  - **Holds:**
    - an empty explanation is refused;
    - the status is kept and ON_HOLD is shown;
    - the customer sees the explanation but never the internal note, and the email is queued;
    - moves and a second hold are refused;
    - resolving emails the customer, and resolving twice is refused.
  - **Deposit, balance and release:**
    - the deposit queues both items;
    - item 1 is finished and becomes READY_AWAITING_RELEASE, with BALANCE_REQUIRED, the balance DUE and its reminder queued; item 2 stays QUEUED, so items move independently;
    - paying the balance releases item 1, and the event row and the PAID installment have the same transaction id (`xmin`), so it happened in the same transaction;
    - item 2 then goes straight to READY_TO_DISPATCH.
  - **Permissions:** staff without PRODUCTION_MANAGE get 403; customers and anonymous visitors get 404; an unknown item gets 404.

## Questions and conflicts
1. **Hold reason categories and "who acts" options** aren't defined in PRD-06 (Pen says "to be decided"). I used: customer information, artwork, materials, equipment, payment, other; and who acts = customer or HolyPearls. Owner to confirm (ATTENTION).
2. **Summary precedence.** PRD-00 §8 lists the summary names but not their order. As built:
   - shipment states come first (delivered, then dispatched);
   - then ON_HOLD, then BALANCE_REQUIRED;
   - then ready, in production, waiting, CONFIRMED.
   A hold on one item doesn't hide that others have left.
3. **The balance falls due when an item passes QC.** It does not wait for the whole order. That is the earliest point the balance blocks dispatch.
4. **Existing orders.** Items that were already AWAITING_PREREQUISITE before this release move on at their next event (an approval or a payment); there is no backfill. Production has no orders yet.
5. **Admin order page.** Pen 114's balance block sits on an order page, which no ticket has built so far. Suggestion: an admin order detail with T07-03.
6. **A change accepted after production started** (T06-03, NEW_PROOF_VERSION) doesn't move the item back. Suggestion: decide this with T07-03 cancellation and scope changes.
