## What was built

- **Branch:** `feature/t12-03-organizer-dashboard`. **PR:** see the tracker row.
- **Migration `0048_collection_review.sql`:**
  - Collections gain `closed_at` and `last_reminded_at`.
  - Participants gain `excluded_at` (left out during review), `removed_at` and `removed_by_user_id`.
- **Contracts (`groups.ts`):** the dashboard (progress, issues, sizes, sizes to confirm, names, pieces, delivery, participants, last reminder, closed at), the participant row, and the correction, settle-size, participant action and reopen inputs.
- **API (`groups/dashboard.ts`):**
  - **Dashboard:**
    - **Progress:** submitted, incomplete, not started, removed and excluded.
    - **Issues:** only for people still in the order. Hasn't sent, size to confirm, or correction asked.
    - **Live size counts** (counting quantities), names, pieces, and delivery by the central location or each approved branch.
    - **Every participant's answers.**
  - **Correction** (while OPEN, for a submitted participant): NEEDS_CORRECTION with a note. They're emailed a fresh personal link and see "Please fix 1 thing" until they send again.
  - **Settle size:** confirms a "not sure" size.
  - **Exclude / include** (during review): leaves a row out of the order or puts it back.
  - **Remove** (any time before an Order Draft):
    - REMOVED, out of the totals, kept in the list and the audit.
    - Their links are revoked and their view becomes 404.
  - **Remind** (while OPEN): emails everyone in the order who hasn't sent a fresh link, and records when.
  - **Close** (OPEN or PAUSED): CLOSED, then straight into review. NEEDS_REVIEW while any issue remains, otherwise READY_TO_ORDER, re-evaluated after every fix. Nothing is ordered or quoted.
  - **Reopen** (CLOSED, NEEDS_REVIEW or READY_TO_ORDER): back to OPEN with a new future deadline. Reopening after an Order Draft is T12-03b.
  - **Every action is audited.** OpenAPI documents the 5 routes.
- **Customer site:**
  - **The collection page** shows the setup while it's a draft. After that it shows the organizer dashboard, with the settings and share link under "Collection settings and share link".
  - **Dashboard (frames 30, 74):**
    - The status line and source.
    - "Remind N people", "Close collection" (with "Closing doesn't start production") and "Reopen" with a new deadline.
    - The big "N / M submitted" with complete, incomplete and not-started chips.
    - "Sizes and delivery so far" with the size table and "Counts are live · not an order until closed and reviewed".
    - The issues list, with "Confirm size" and, during review, "Leave out".
    - Participant filters (All, Not started, Incomplete, Complete, Removed), and per row: Ask to correct, Put back, and Remove with a confirmation (frame 134 without payment).
    - NEEDS_REVIEW and READY_TO_ORDER banners (frame 133).

## Permission rules added

- **The dashboard:** the same people who can see the collection (creator, `MANAGE_COLLECTIONS` or `VIEW_RECORDS` for the location).
- **Every action needs `MANAGE_COLLECTIONS` for the location.** Viewers get 403, and participants' guest sessions get 401.
- **A removed participant can't reach their submission** (404), and their links are revoked.

## Differences from the design or PRD, and why

1. **"HP-G-2026-0019" reference (frame 30):** collections don't have a reference number yet. The title, source and location identify it.
2. **Per-issue "Suggest 'BUKOLA'" and "unsupported characters" (frame 30):** names are already limited to 12 characters when entered, so those issues can't arise. "Size to confirm" has an inline size picker.
3. **"Send reminder" per person (frame 30):** reminders go to everyone who hasn't sent ("Remind N people"); one-person reminders can follow.
4. **"Export CSV" and "CSV import · 114 of 120 rows valid" (frame 30):** CSV import arrives with T12-03c.
5. **CLOSED is passed through straight to review:** TECH-01 lists CLOSED before NEEDS_REVIEW and READY_TO_ORDER. Closing records CLOSED in the audit and then sets the review status, so organizers see what to fix immediately (frame 133's states follow the close confirmation directly).
6. **Removing (frame 134) has no payment line:** participants never pay (owner decision 2026-10-10).
