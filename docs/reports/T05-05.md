## What was built

### Quote page `/quotes/[id]` (Pen 25 desktop, 46 mobile, 47 expired)
- **The page shows:**
  - status ("Quote V2 · ready for your decision"), the reference, the version and valid-until;
  - **version history**;
  - **What changed from V1** (QUOTE-FR-005), worked out from the two versions: quantities and prices, lines added or removed, fees, total;
  - the lines with each unit price, the fees and VAT;
  - **What happens after you accept** (deposit, proofs, production, the scheduled payments, delivery);
  - payment terms, terms, assumptions and the production sequence;
  - the dark **payment summary** (total, and each payment with its percentage and trigger), the validity and the decision box.
- **Actions:**
  - **Accept** needs the review tick ("I have reviewed the scope…") and an **email code** if none was checked in the last 24 hours. The existing code dialog (TECH-03 §5) opens and accepting continues by itself.
  - **Request changes** (a line or the whole quote, the kind of change and a note): the quote goes to REVISION_REQUESTED.
  - **Ask a question** (the "Your HolyPearls contact" card).
  - **Decline**, with an optional reason.
- **Expired** (Pen 47): read only, with "This quote expired on …" and **Request updated quote**, which moves it back to PENDING_REVIEW and keeps the project and history. Accept is unavailable.
- **Access:**
  - the quote's link, the account, or whoever can open its project or design (owner, browser or link);
  - anyone else gets 404;
  - never indexed or cached.

### Accepting (QUOTE-FR-007–008, TECH-05 §8)
- **Blockers** are checked when accepting:
  - not READY;
  - not the current SENT version;
  - past valid-until;
  - no recent email code;
  - opened without a session (the quote's email link is needed).
- **In one transaction, under the quote's lock:**
  - an insert-only **acceptance record**;
  - the version and quote become ACCEPTED, the project (or design) ORDERED;
  - the **order is made from that version's lines** (`orders.quote_version_id` is unique), with items AWAITING_PREREQUISITE;
  - the **payment schedule and installments** are created from the version's schedule. Payments on acceptance or before production are DUE; before dispatch and dated ones are NOT_DUE;
  - a dated payment gets its `reminder.send` job;
  - a **"Quote accepted"** email goes out with the order's link and what's due now;
  - it's audited.
- **Pressing more than once,** or several presses at once, gives the same order.

### Deposits and balances (PAY-FR-001–004)
- **The order page** for an order from a quote shows a **payment summary**:
  - total, paid and remaining;
  - each payment with its trigger, date and status;
  - **Pay [next payment]**.
- **Paying** opens Paystack with the same attempt flow as checkout.
  - The common part of T04-03's Pay is now `beginAttempt`, used by both.
  - Only the **next unpaid payment** can be paid; paying early is allowed.
  - While a payment is being confirmed the page checks again every 5 seconds and never shows "Paid" early (PAY-FR-009).
- **A successful payment** marks the installment PAID and makes a **receipt** (PAY-FR-006). The customer gets **"Deposit received"** (or the balance's name) with what's left.
- **The customer's payment status** follows the schedule (PRD-00 §8): DEPOSIT_DUE, DEPOSIT_PAID, BALANCE_DUE, PAID…
- **A dated payment:** `reminder.send INSTALLMENT_DUE` marks it DUE and emails "payment due".
- **"Before dispatch" payments** become due when T07-01 releases finished items.

### Staff
- **The quote builder** lists what the customer sent about the quote (OPS-FR-034): changes, with line and type; questions; declines; renewal requests.

### Data (migration `0030_quote_acceptance`)
- **New tables:** `quote_change_requests`, `quote_acceptances` (insert-only, unique per version).
- **New columns:** `payment_schedules.quote_version_id` and `orders.quote_version_id`, both unique, each with "checkout or quote version, exactly one"; `installments.due_on`.
- **Emails:** `quote_accepted`, `payment_due` and `payment_received`, all with secure links.

## Questions and conflicts
1. **Accepting needs a session:** a visitor who only has the project in their browser is asked to open the quote from the email link. Email codes need a customer, which the link's session provides. Signed-in owners accept directly.
2. **Order totals:** for an order from a quote, the order's goods include the design, installation and other fees; delivery is kept separately and VAT is the version's. Each item's VAT is a share of its line, but the version's VAT total is the figure charged.
3. **"Before dispatch" balances** stay NOT_DUE until production releases items (T07-01). Customers can pay early.
4. **Browser test gap:** the end-to-end browser test doesn't accept or pay, because the e2e run has no email worker to read the code from. Acceptance and payment are fully covered by the API tests. Suggestion: an e2e mail catcher later.
5. **Money symbol:** amounts on the customer pages use ₦. USD quotes need USD switched on, and would show the wrong symbol until a currency-aware formatter is shared (small follow-up).
