## What was built

### Database (migrations `0011_product_rules_prices`, `0012_price_history_insert_only`)
- **`product_rules`**: one row per product, versioned (optimistic locking) and audited. It holds:
  - MOQ and what happens below it: BLOCK, MANUAL_REVIEW or SURCHARGE with a small-run charge in basis points (OPS-FR-011)
  - an automatic-pricing quantity limit (above it, QUOTE_REQUIRED)
  - lead time range in working days; rush and priority rush, each NONE, AVAILABLE with a surcharge, or MANUAL_REVIEW (OPS-FR-012)
  - artwork-later (OPS-FR-016)
  - samples: not offered, optional, recommended or required, priced as the larger of unit price × multiplier and a minimum (OPS-FR-017)
  - proof not required, optional or required, and whether an exact reorder can skip it (OPS-FR-018)
  - `check` constraints: MOQ ≥ 1, lead max ≥ min, basis points in range, limit ≥ MOQ
- **`compatibility_rules`** (OPS-FR-013): "when option X is V, option Y can't be (or needs review for) these values", with the customer-facing message. The set is replaced as a whole; saving bumps the product rules' version so each calculation records which set it used. Rules must name real option values (validated).
- **`price_rules`** (OPS-FR-010): prices from an effective date, in minor units plus currency. They hold:
  - basis PER_UNIT, PER_PACK (with pack size, for example ₦15,000 per 100 cards) or FIXED (a whole-order price)
  - a base amount
  - quantity tiers
  - option add-ons, per unit or per order
  - PRICE_READY or ESTIMATE_READY (OPS-FR-015)
  - whether the price includes VAT (OPS-FR-019)
- **`tax_settings`** (OPS-FR-019): VAT rate in basis points from an effective date, and whether VAT applies to delivery.
- **Insert-only history:** a database trigger refuses UPDATE and DELETE on `price_rules` and `tax_settings` (tested). A change is a new row with a later date, and a past date is refused. So effective dates are respected and a past calculation can always name the rules it used.
- **`price_results`** (configuration module): every calculation's input, result, total, state and the exact price rule, product-rules version and tax setting used. Insert-only (UPDATE refused). The daily clean-up removes results older than 30 days, because carts and quotes will keep their own snapshots (TECH-01 §5).

### The calculator (`apps/api/src/configuration/calculate.ts`)
- **A pure function:** product rules + compatibility + price rule + VAT + choices + quantity + rush + sample + delivery → the result below. No database, no clock, no floating point: money is `bigint` minor units, percentages are basis points, and there is one rounding helper (`mulDivRound`, half up).
- **The result includes:**
  - goods lines (base, each add-on, small-run charge, rush), goods total, delivery, VAT, total and **per unit** (COM-FR-005)
  - the price state and whether the configuration can be ordered
  - reasons with customer-facing messages
  - lead days (shortened for rush), the proof rule and artwork-later
  - the rule versions used
- **Price state** follows the worst condition:
  - CONSULTATIVE_PROJECT products → CONSULTATION_REQUIRED, with no amount
  - CONFIGURABLE_QUOTE products, or an estimate-only price → ESTIMATE_READY
  - a choice combination that needs review, below MOQ with manual review, above the automatic limit, rush needing review, a missing price, rules or VAT, or delivery in another currency → **QUOTE_REQUIRED, keeping every choice** (COM-FR-060)
  - otherwise PRICE_READY
- **Blocking reasons** mark the result not valid: a missing or unknown choice, choices that aren't allowed together, below MOQ when blocked, rush not offered, samples not offered, a bad quantity.
- **Order of charges:** tiered goods, add-ons, sample price, small-run charge, rush, delivery, VAT. Each percentage is rounded half up at its own step. Inclusive prices extract VAT (`amount × rate ÷ (10,000 + rate)`); exclusive prices add it. VAT covers delivery when the setting says so.
- **Currencies are never converted** (TECH-05 §2): delivery in another currency turns the result into QUOTE_REQUIRED instead.

### Service and API
- `priceProduct` loads the rules **in force at the request's time** (the actor's clock, so tests can move time), calculates and saves the result. `pricePublishedProduct` does the same for published products by page address.
- Public `POST /v1/catalogue/products/:slug/price` (SEARCH rate limit): choices, quantity, rush, sample. It returns the saved result, with amounts as strings of minor units. The browser may show it; checkout will recalculate on the server (TECH-05 §1).
- **Admin, all PRICING_MANAGE (403 for other staff, 404 for customers), every change audited:**
  - `GET/PUT /v1/admin/pricing/products/:id/rules` (version 0 creates)
  - `PUT …/compatibility`
  - `GET/POST …/prices` (history, with the price in force marked `current`; scheduled prices appear before it)
  - `POST …/calculate` (staff can try unpublished products)
  - `GET/POST /v1/admin/pricing/vat`
- The admin screens are out of scope ("Not in this ticket: Screens"). The "Rules & prices" nav item still shows its placeholder.

## Questions / conflicts found

- **Illustrative prices:** the Stage 1.4 sheet gives one starting price per product. The tiers, size and finish add-ons in the seed are invented so the calculator has realistic data to show, and each is marked illustrative. **Needed before launch:** real tier and add-on prices (ATTENTION list).
- **Small-run charge:** the sheet gives a range (20–40%, product specific). The seed uses 30% where small runs are allowed. **Confirm the per-product values.**
- **"Most ordered" prices on cards:** the product card still shows the price state, not "From ₦…". The public card and product page start using the calculator in T03-03 (the product page and configurator), which is its natural place.
- **Saved results without an owner:** public estimates save a result each time (TECH-01 requires saved results). They're rate limited (SEARCH class) and cleaned up after 30 days. Once carts and quotes exist, they keep snapshots; if they should reference `price_results` rows instead, the clean-up must skip referenced rows.
- **VAT on delivery:** set to yes in the seed. Nigerian VAT generally applies to delivery services, but **confirm with the accountant**.
