**API**
- **Sign-up:** the Better Auth email and password sign-up route is opened on the customer site only. Staff accounts stay STAFF_ADMIN-only, and the admin surface refuses sign-up.
- **Email verification:**
  - A link is sent on sign-up. Opening it signs the person in.
  - An unverified account can sign in and sees "Verify your email"; nothing is linked until it verifies.
  - `/verify-email` and `/send-verification-email` are added to the allowed auth routes.
- **Password reset:**
  - The reset link is emailed.
  - The request always answers the same way, so it never reveals whether an account exists.
  - Saving a new password ends every other session (`revokeSessionsOnPasswordReset`).
- **Email templates:** `verify_email` and `password_reset`. Both carry secrets, so they are sealed and never resent.
- **Guest linking:**
  - When an account's email becomes verified, by the verification link or a magic link, every `customers` row with that exact email joins the account (`linkCustomersToVerifiedEmail`). An audit entry records it.
  - Orders, quotes, projects, proofs and receipts already reach accounts through `customers.userId`, so linking the row links all of them.
- **Rate limits (AUTH):** sign-in, magic link, sign-up, request reset, reset password and resend verification share the AUTH limit.
- **`GET /v1/me`:** gains `emailVerified` and `phone`.
- **`PATCH /v1/me/profile`:** name and phone. The name goes to the account and to its customer record, the phone to the customer record. Email change is not in this ticket (see below).
- **`GET /v1/me/history`:** the overview is built from each module's own exported functions (TECH-00 §5).
  - **Drafts to resume:** configuration drafts and project drafts.
  - **Lists:** orders, quotes and projects, and receipts.
  - **Linking status:** unverified; verified with no earlier guest records; or verified recently with N orders and M quotes linked.

**Web (customer site)**
- **`/sign-in`** (frame 87): "Forgot password?" and "Create an account" go to the new pages. After sign-in the default destination is `/account`.
- **`/sign-up`** (frame 88): sign-up, plus the verify-pending, verified, already-registered and link-expired states.
- **`/reset-password`** (frame 89): request, sent (the same message for any email), set new password, token expired, success.
- **`/account`** (frame 92): overview with the "just linked", unverified, nothing-to-link, empty, loading and error states.
- **`/account/profile`** (frame 91): name and phone; the email is shown read-only. Saving, saved, field error and save-failed states.
- **`/account/security`** (frame 90): adds the change-password form. It uses the shared account navigation.
- **Account navigation:** Orders, Quotes, Projects, Drafts and Receipts go to their sections on the overview. There are no separate list pages; the Pen file only draws the overview.
- **Design system:** tokens from `packages/ui/tokens/tokens.json` and components from `packages/ui`.

**Not in this ticket (follow-up T09-01b):**

## Permission rules added
- **History and profile** are the signed-in person's own, and a signed-out request gets 401. The overview only reads records owned by the account (`ownerUserId`) or by its linked customer record.
- **An unverified account links nothing.** Its email's guest record keeps no `userId`, a guest order placed with that email stays 404, and a phone can't be written to a record it doesn't own (RULE_FAILED `VERIFY_EMAIL_FIRST`).
- **The admin never serves sign-up or the email check** (ADR-017).
- **A sign-in link never creates an account.**

## Differences from the design or PRD, and why
- **Marketing consent:** the sign-up checkbox and the profile "Email preferences" toggle are not built. Consent storage is T15-03's ("[was T15-04] … marketing consent"), and a toggle that saves nothing would be wrong.
- **Terms and privacy line:** the sign-up line "By creating an account you agree to the Terms. Read our Privacy notice." is left out. Neither page exists yet; the privacy notice is T15-03.
- **Email change:** the Pen draws an email change with an "Email change pending" state. It is split into T09-01b, so for now the email is read-only with "To change the email on your account, contact us."
- **After a reset:** the Pen's success state says "You're signed in here". Better Auth's reset doesn't sign the person in, and the reset link doesn't carry the email. So the state says "All your other sessions were signed out. Sign in with your new password." with a Sign in button. Every other session still ends (TECH-03 §4).
- **Account sub-pages:** Orders, Quotes, Projects, Drafts and Receipts in the account nav go to the overview's sections. The Pen draws only the overview; list pages are T09-01b if needed.
- **Receipt rows:** they show the amount, the Paystack reference and the date. The Pen's "Deposit" label isn't stored on receipts.
- **Page titles:** they use the site's heading style, as the security page from T00-06 already did. The Pen's page title is the UI font at 40 px.
- **"Just linked" banner:** it shows for 14 days after verification. The Pen doesn't say how long; after that, linked records are simply in the lists.
