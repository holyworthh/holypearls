## Differences from the design or PRD, and why

1. **Freight rates are in naira.** Frame 119's example is "$45 per shipment up to 10 kg (test)". Delivery rates are NGN and the calculator never converts currencies (TECH-05 §2). Weight bands aren't built: a group has one rate per shipment.
2. **"Fixed per product" is a fee for each piece installed.** Travel is once per order.
3. **The survey rule uses the custom size** (width × height). Standard sizes are all well under 6 m². The frame says "wall graphics"; the rule applies to any installed graphic over the size.
4. **Installation rule changes reach a saved draft on its next change.** Opening a draft reprices it only after price, product-rules or VAT changes (T13-01b).
5. **Countries are typed as codes** ("GH, TG, BJ") rather than picked from a list.
6. **Quotes:** a quoted installation reaches staff as the draft's price reasons (state, site, survey or notice). Staff still set the installation fee on the quote, as before (T05).
