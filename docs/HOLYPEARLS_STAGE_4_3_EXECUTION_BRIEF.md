# HOLYPEARLS — STAGE 4.3
## Content, Asset Production & Implementation Design Handoff

**Status:** Execution brief  
**Phase:** Post–high-fidelity content/asset/handoff stage  
**Prerequisite:** Stage 4.2 is frozen and approved  
**Next phase after this:** Technical architecture/security/backend specifications, then implementation

---

# 0. PURPOSE

Stage 4.2 solved the interface-design problem.

Stage 4.3 must now make the approved HolyPearls system **real, factual, photographically credible, content-complete, operationally grounded, and implementation-ready**.

This is **not another visual redesign**.

Do **not**:
- restart art direction;
- change Geist / Geist Mono;
- redesign the Stage 4.1/4.2 visual system;
- invent new page architecture for variety;
- begin coding;
- begin backend/security architecture;
- convert unconfirmed test values into production truth;
- use generated imagery as evidence of real HolyPearls capability;
- fabricate clients, facilities, people, orders, case studies, machines, or results.

The objective is to bridge:

> **FROZEN HIGH-FIDELITY DESIGN**  
> → **REAL CONTENT + REAL ASSETS + REAL BUSINESS INPUTS**  
> → **IMPLEMENTATION DESIGN HANDOFF**

# 3. CORE RULES

## 3.1 Real evidence beats beautiful placeholders

Anything that makes a factual claim about HolyPearls should ultimately be backed by HolyPearls evidence.

Examples:

- production floor;
- sewing/cutting;
- embroidery;
- printing;
- large-format output;
- QC;
- packing;
- real finished products;
- real case-study work;
- real staff/operators;
- real installation where offered.

Generated imagery may remain for clearly marked demo/system content.

It must not become proof of a capability that has not been photographed or verified.

---

## 3.2 Do not guess business truth

If HolyPearls has not confirmed a value, use a status.

Allowed:

- `CONFIRMED BY BUSINESS`
- `PROVISIONAL RESEARCH`
- `TEST DATA`
- `NOT YET PROVIDED`
- `NOT APPLICABLE`

Do not silently turn:

- prototype price;
- researched market price;
- assumed MOQ;
- assumed turnaround;
- assumed shipping time;
- assumed stock;
- assumed production method

into launch content.

---

## 3.3 Shared templates are correct; duplicate filler is not

The 314-page catalogue should continue using Stage 4.2 archetypes.

Do not create 314 new layouts.

But every launch product must have useful product-specific content:

- identity;
- relevant imagery;
- what it is;
- who it is for;
- configuration guidance;
- materials/specs;
- price state;
- MOQ;
- lead-time state;
- proof/sample rules;
- artwork requirements;
- use cases;
- related products/projects/resources;
- page-specific SEO metadata.

---

## 3.4 Protect provenance

Every external or internally produced asset should have a known source.

Track:

- creator;
- copyright owner;
- client/property;
- usage rights;
- model release;
- property/location approval;
- stock/supplier license;
- generated/AI status;
- launch permission;
- source/evidence link.

Unknown rights = **not launch-ready**.

## 6.4 Capture master policy

Do not capture to final web dimensions.

Require:

- highest practical native camera resolution;
- RAW masters where available;
- high-resolution edited master;
- accurate color;
- consistent white balance;
- consistent framing within product families;
- no destructive crop as the only master;
- no text overlay baked into main product images.

Web derivatives come later.

---

## 6.5 Required crop planning

Evaluate important assets for:

- `1:1`
- `4:5`
- `4:3`
- `16:9` or `16:10`
- `9:16` only when a real mobile/video need exists

Do not assume a single crop will work everywhere.

---

## 6.6 Shoot preparation

Create a shoot-prep checklist:

- products cleaned;
- clothing steamed;
- lint/dust removed;
- real approved artwork selected;
- print samples prepared;
- machines/stations cleaned enough to photograph;
- staff consent/release ready;
- client/location approval ready;
- lighting/backdrop plan;
- props prepared;
- hero order;
- macro order;
- process order;
- lifestyle order;
- install/project order.

Group similar products/setups to maintain consistent lighting and framing.
